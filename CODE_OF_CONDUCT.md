# Code of conduct

This project collects what people have said about Atatürk over a century, including things many readers will find offensive or wrong. Working on it means discussing contested history with people who disagree. These rules apply in every project space: issues, pull requests, comments, commit messages and discussions.

## Disagree about history, with sources

- Disagreement about history is expected. Say what you think is wrong and why.
- Sources settle questions, not the number of people who agree or how strongly they feel. If you dispute a record, point to the source, the page and the passage.
- Argue about the record, never about the person you are talking to.

## No harassment and no insults

- No insults, threats, slurs or personal attacks against anyone.
- This includes historical figures. Atatürk, his opponents, and every voice in the archive are discussed by what the sources say, not insulted. Hostile historical quotations belong in records, with their sources; they do not belong in your comments.
- No harassment of contributors, in project spaces or elsewhere because of their work here.
- Do not post other people's private information.

## The maintainer decides

The maintainer reviews every contribution and decides what is merged. The maintainer may edit, hide or delete comments, close or lock issues and pull requests, and block people from the project, without a long debate. A contribution that breaks these rules will be closed.

## Contact

To report a problem, open an issue, or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile: [github.com/okanerturan](https://github.com/okanerturan). If the problem involves the maintainer, say so; it will be read.
