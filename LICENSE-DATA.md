## How to attribute

Use a line like this one, in the place where you show or describe the data:

> Data: Okan Erturan and contributors, *How the world saw Atatürk*, https://github.com/okanerturan/how-the-world-saw-ataturk, licensed under CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

If you changed the data (filtered it, corrected it, translated it), add "adapted" and say what you changed.

## Quotations

The `quote` fields contain the words of the people and publications being recorded. Those words remain theirs. They are reproduced here as short quotations, for documentation and research, each with its source. The CC BY 4.0 licence covers our selection, our summaries, our notes, our translations and the structure of the records. It does not give you rights in the quoted words beyond what the law already allows for quotation. When you reuse a quotation, keep its source with it.
