## Seam notes
- `https://archive.org/advancedsearch.php` works but the raw "Kemal"/"Angora" query is flooded by newspaper-collection pages; `https://archive.org/services/search/beta/page_production/?service_backend=fts&user_query=…` (full-text hits, 100 per page) found most of the books above.
- For open items, `https://<server>/fulltext/inside.php?item_id=<id>&doc=<id>&path=<dir>&q=<term>` (server and dir from `/metadata/<id>`) returns each hit with its leaf number (`page` = n<k>) — enough to build `details/<id>/page/n<k>?q=` links. It answers 403 for restricted items, so no lending-library snippet was taken.
- Printed page = leaf − offset (Huddleston 8, Morgenthau 12, Horton 1926 scan 26) — offset differs by scan; check one chapter heading against the table of contents.
- Not tried: HathiTrust full-view and Project Gutenberg (no English book above is Gutenberg-only; HathiTrust duplicates of IA scans are likely).
