# Candidates — before 1919

Leads from the search of 10 November 2026 for texts about Mustafa Kemal that existed before 19 May 1919. Eleven voices from that search are records already (see [`../LEDGER.md`](../LEDGER.md), round 36). Everything below is a lead, not a finding: it still needs the source named in the last column.

## Tested and dry

- A teacher's or classmate's words from his school years: nothing written at the time was found. The story of the maths teacher and the name "Kemal" is his own later account.
- Sofia, 1913–14: no trace in the Austrian press of those years; the only mention is the 1916 telegram.
- The Kaiser's greeting ("16th Corps, Anafarta"): his own later story as far as could be found.
- Italian diplomatic documents for January–June 1919: no mention.

## Namesakes to watch for

An Ottoman naval attaché of the same name (Athens 1915–16, Vienna 1917–18); the Egyptian nationalist Mustafa Kamil Pasha (died 1908); "Exzellenz Kemal Pascha", a character in the operetta *Die Rose von Stambul* (1916–18).
