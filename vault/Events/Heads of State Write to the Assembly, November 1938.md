# Heads of State Write to the Assembly, November 1938

The first condolence cables reached Ankara within hours of 10 November 1938, addressed to the Speaker,
**Abdülhalik Renda**, who was acting head of state, and pasted into two volumes by the Assembly's library.
[[Condolence Telegrams to Ankara, November 1938]] maps the whole collection from the typed index and
the OCR. This note is the **sheet-by-sheet reading of the notable senders against the page images**: who
wrote, in what words, and which of them actually *characterised* the man rather than recited the formula.
(PDF pages; vol. 1 = c1, vol. 2 = c2.)

## The rule

A pure protocol line (*"profound condolences on the loss"*, *"illustrious president"*, *"great president"*)
is not a gaze. A sender counts only where the telegram says what Atatürk **was or did**. Honorifics
attached to the office (*illustre*, *éminent*, *gran presidente*) are protocol; a noun for his achievement
(*founder*, *regenerator*, *genius*, *architect of the rebirth*) is a gaze.

## Who characterised him

- **Albert Lebrun, President of the French Republic (c2 p. 49, Paris, 10 Nov).** *"C'est avec une émotion
  profonde que je m'associe au deuil de la Turquie au moment où disparaît l'ardent patriote et le grand
  homme d'état qui avait consacré sa vie à la glorieuse résurrection de sa patrie. Son œuvre, réalisée par
  de saines et pacifiques méthodes, marquera dans l'histoire des peuples."* ("...the ardent patriot and
  great statesman who had devoted his life to the glorious resurrection of his homeland. His work,
  achieved by sound and peaceful methods, will leave its mark on the history of peoples", tr.) The only
  head of state who praises the *method*.
- **The Swiss Federal Council, President Baumann (c2 p. 19, Bern, 10 Nov).** *"...au génie duquel la
  Turquie moderne doit son admirable essor"* ("to whose genius modern Turkey owes her admirable rise",
  tr.). The one "genius" in the head-of-state cables. (Baumann's first name, Johannes, is from memory;
  the telegram signs "Baumann, président de la Confédération suisse".)
- **Prince Paul, Regent of Yugoslavia (c2 p. 10, Belgrade, 10 Nov; the index calls him "King").**
  *"...Kemal Atatürk, le grand chef et réformateur de la nation turque"* and the grief of "Turquie notre
  chère et noble alliée" ("the great leader and reformer of the Turkish nation ... our dear and noble
  ally", tr.).
- **The Yugoslav Senate, president "Dr Zlimir Mažuranić" as telegraphed (c2 p. 11, Belgrade, 10 Nov).**
  *"...la mort du grand homme d'état et père de la patrie"* ("the great statesman and father of the
  fatherland", tr.), and Yugoslavia "n'oubliera jamais son ami sincère et fidèle". The surname is as the
  sheet prints it; the Senate president's identity is not independently checked here.
- **Stefan Ćirić, president of the Yugoslav Chamber of Deputies (c2 p. 12, Belgrade, 10 Nov).** The
  strongest sentence in the set. *"Je déplore non seulement un des plus grands hommes [de l']histoire
  mondiale, non seulement un des plus grands hommes d'état, régénérateur et créateur, mais en plus
  grand ami qui collabora fidèlement avec [le] roi martyr Alexandre l'unificateur..."* ("not only one of
  the greatest men of world history, one of the greatest statesmen, regenerator and creator, but also
  the great friend who collaborated faithfully with the martyred King Alexander the Unifier", tr.).
  Compare [[Yugoslav Press, November 1938]].
- **Luigi Federzoni, President of the Italian Senate (c1 p. 7; transmitted by the Italian embassy in
  Ankara on 12 Nov, c1 p. 6).** *"Senato del Regno ha appreso con vivo cordoglio immatura scomparsa
  presidente Repubblica Kemal Atatürk artefice del rinnovamento della nazione..."* ("the Senate
  of the Kingdom learned with deep sorrow the untimely death of ... the *architect of the renewal of the
  nation*", tr.). **Costanzo Ciano, Chamber of Deputies (c1 p. 8)** on the paired sheet: *"artefice
  della rinascita della nazione turca"* (the architect of the rebirth of the Turkish nation).
  The same noun (*artefice*) in two hands on the same day.
- **Nahhas Pasha, Wafd leader (c2 p. 26, Cairo, 12 Nov).** Beyond the formula: *"...pour perte
  prématurée grand homme Turquie et Orient Atatürk. Son nom, son œuvre vivront pour toujours"* ("for the
  premature loss of a great man of Turkey and of the East ... his name, his work will live for ever",
  tr.), followed by congratulations to İnönü and good wishes for "grand peuple turc, sœur Égypte". This
  upgrades the earlier index-level record, which had no quote.
- **Manuel Azaña, President of the Spanish Republic (c2 p. 10, Barcelona).** *"A l'occasion du décès du
  fondateur de la République turque Kemal Atatürk veuillez accepter avec mes plus sincères condoléances
  les meilleurs vœux pour la continuité de sa grande œuvre"* ("founder of the Turkish Republic ... best
  wishes for the continuity of his great work", tr.). The earlier record was second-hand (Tüylü Turan);
  this is the sheet. See [[Two Spains Mourn, November 1938]].
- **C. Mercouriou, Mayor of Thessaloniki (c1 p. 11).** *"...mort son illustre enfant Kemal Atatürk
  réformateur nouvelle turque [Turquie] et ami fidèle Grèce"* ("his illustrious child, reformer of the new Turkey
  and faithful friend of Greece", tr.): a Salonikan mourning the native son. See
  [[Salonika Names His Street, November 1938]].
- **Prof. Hermann Jansen, the planner of Ankara (c2 p. 15, Berlin).** *"Herzliches Beileid zum schweren
  Verlust des hochgenialen Staatspräsidenten Kemal Atatürk — tiefbewegt Professor Hermann Jansen"*
  ("the highly ingenious state president", tr.). A rare cable from someone who built what Atatürk
  commissioned.
- **Sir George Clerk (c2 p. 43, London).** *"...your grief at the loss of the great man whose memory will
  ever be immortal in your hearts."* The role is not on the sheet; the index says "London, George
  Clerk" and the name fits the former British ambassador in Ankara (from memory).
- **Romanian women's associations, Hortense de Satmary (c2 p. 24, Bucharest).** *"...la mort du grand
  victorieux Kamal Atatürk, saveur [sic: sauveur] de sa nation et libérateur des femmes turques"* ("saviour of his
  nation and liberator of Turkish women", tr.).

## Re-read and confirmed

Roosevelt (c1 p. 35: *"whose devotion to his country won for him the respect of the entire world"*);
[[Choudhry Rahmat Ali]] (c1 p. 36: *"Turkey has lost its maker, Asia its heroic apostle of nationalism and
the world one of its greatest statesmen"*); Farouk (c2 p. 6, in French: *"l'illustre patriote régénérateur
de sa Turquie nouvelle et national[e]"*); Lyons (c2 p. 28: *"the great loss sustained by them in the death of
their illustrious statesman and founder of the Turkish Republic"*); Husseini (c2 p. 21, French, signed
"Amin Husseni"); the Egyptian Chamber (c2 p. 26: *"glorious hero, ... renovator and creator"*); Isaac
Ben-Zvi for the Jewish General Council of Palestine (c2 p. 44: *"great founder regenerator new Turkey"*);
Stoycho Moshanov (c1 p. 14, French: *"de son renovateur et grand chef d'état"*). Two corrections follow
from the images: **Hitler's** cable (c2 p. 14) is in German and speaks of *"des neuen türkischen
Reiches"* (the new Turkish **Reich**, not "state"): *"ein grosser Soldat, ein genialer Staatsmann und eine
geschichtliche Persönlichkeit ... In der Errichtung des neuen türkischen Reiches hat sich Atatürk ein
Denkmal gesetzt, dessen Bestand die Generationen überdauern wird"*. And **Hirohito's** (c1 p. 28) is in
**French** (*"c'est avec une vive affliction que j'apprends la mort de S. E. Kemal Atatürk"*), so the English
line the earlier note printed is a translation.

## Protocol only (they wrote; the words do not characterise)

Wrote and said nothing about the man: [[Ibn Saud]] (Abdulaziz, Riyadh, c1 p. 9, Arabic in Latin letters),
Ulmanis (c1 p. 3), Tsar Boris III (c1 p. 13), Gustaf V (c1 p. 16), Päts (c1 p. 17), King Zog (c1 p. 25),
Prince Takamatsu (c1 p. 27; see [[Prince Takamatsu]]), Mościcki (c1 p. 32), Abbas Hilmi II (c1 p. 37),
Göring for the Reichstag (c2 p. 15: *"ihres großen Präsidenten"*, an honorific on the office, treated like
Cárdenas's *"su gran presidente"* and Atassi's *"éminent chef"*), Carol II (c2 p. 23), Horthy (c2 p. 25),
Hashim al-Atassi (c2 p. 29), Zahir Shah (c2 p. 39, Persian in Latin letters), Christian X (c2 p. 41),
Grand Duchess Charlotte and Cárdenas (c2 p. 45), Haakon VII (c2 p. 48), Victor Emmanuel III (c2 p. 53),
[[Abdullah I of Jordan]] (c2 p. 7), and the Hatay head of state and Assembly president (c2 pp. 4–5).

## Not found

- **Kalinin** (USSR): in the index only; no sheet in c2 (the Moscow sheet, c2 p. 46, is the Turkish
  ambassador's).
- **Indian National Congress / Subhas Chandra Bose:** no such sheet. The index entry "Hindistan, Kongre
  Başkanı" matches the **Zoutpansberg Indian Congress** (Louis Trichardt, Transvaal; c2 p. 44, president
  signing, no name legible) that sits next to the Israel Council sheet in the same order. Nothing in the
  volumes ties [[Subhas Chandra Bose]] to the cable.
