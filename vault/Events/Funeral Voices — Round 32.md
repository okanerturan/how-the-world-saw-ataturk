# Funeral Voices — Round 32

Voices from the weeks of Atatürk's death and funeral (10 November 1938 to January 1939) found in rounds 32–33 and verified at source.

## Ernst Reuter, 1938-11-22
*German Social Democrat in exile, teaching in Ankara; later Governing Mayor of West Berlin* — admiring, letter, evidence P.

Wrote his mother after the burial that he had filed past Atatürk's coffin with his school's staff; everyone felt the greatness of the loss.

> Es ist eine große und allgemeine Trauer, und jeder fühlt wohl die Größe des Verlustes, die gemildert wird dadurch, daß die Nachfolgerfrage ganz glatt und selbstverständlich gelöst worden … [translation: It is a great and general mourning, and everyone surely feels the greatness of the loss, which is softened by the succession question having been settled quite smoothly and as a matter of course.]

Mediated by: Reuter, Schriften – Reden, Bd. 2 (letters 1922–1946, ed. Hirschfeld), letter No. 106 'An die Mutter, Ankara, den [22. November 1938]', read as archive.org search-inside snippets.

⚠️ Lending-only scan (schriftenreden0000reut_d8g2, catalogued as the 1972 set); page not shown. The letter heading and the Atatürk passage ('Gestern haben wir Atatürk beerdigt, und vorgestern bin ich im Lehrkörper meiner Schule an dem Sarg vorbeidefiliert') come from separate snippets, so their being in the same letter is inferred from the text running on. The date is the editors' bracketed date and fits the Ankara ceremony of 21 Nov 1938. A short private remark: the view is of a shared loss, not a judgement of Kemal's work. Check: p. 497; overlapping snippets show the heading and the Atatürk passage are one letter.
