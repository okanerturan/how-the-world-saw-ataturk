# Austrian Nationalrat and Bundesrat on Atatürk, 1980–2019 — Schwaiger, Längle, Cap, Lugar

Seven statements about Atatürk in the stenographic protocols of the Austrian parliament, found by a full scan of the protocols that parlament.gv.at publishes (Konstituierende Nationalversammlung, Nationalrat and Bundesrat sittings, committee extracts and other plenary-type sittings, 1918 to 2025: 6,853 documents, scanned by downloading each PDF or HTML protocol and searching for Atatürk, Kemal, Kemalisten, Angora and Ghasi). The scan found 19 documents with a hit (plus one false OCR hit), of which seven state a view of Atatürk. The only famous speaker is Ottokar Czernin (1921), who names Kemal Pascha in a foreign-policy speech; the later records are modern backbench speeches in headscarf and Turkey-accession debates. Nothing was found for Seipel, Renner, Dollfuss, Schuschnigg, Bauer, Kreisky, Waldheim, Haider or Kurz (Kurz is mentioned in 2017 only as a comparison in an FPÖ speech). Dropped for want of a stated view: Belakowitsch-Jenewein (31 Jan 2017, Atatürk 'managed' the headscarf ban in the 1920s, 'Kurz does not': a comparison), a 1988 joke about Atatürk's six-day speech, a 1954 report of Tolomei's remark about solving South Tyrol 'as radically as Kemal', and a 1999 remark that the Kemalists set out to build a state of Kurds and Turks.

## Bundesrat Dr. Schwaiger — 1980-12-04

Opposing a 15-million-dollar loan to Turkey's central bank, says Atatürk's attempt to make a modern state of Turkey 'had only partial success'.

> Der Versuch Kemal Atatürks, einen modernen Staat aus der Türkei zu machen, hatte nur teilweise Erfolg.
>
> [Translation:] Kemal Atatürk's attempt to make a modern state out of Turkey had only partial success.

**Source.** Bundesrat, 403rd sitting, 4 December 1980, Stenographisches Protokoll p. 14728 — <https://www.parlament.gv.at/dokument/BR/BRSITZ/403/imfname_177961.pdf>

**Caveat.** One evaluative sentence inside an economic argument (the ÖVP opposed the loan). First name not given in the protocol; the page number is that of the sitting's page before the running head '14729 Dr. Schwaiger'. Not a notable voice.

## Christoph Längle — 2015-03-12

In a headscarf and secularism debate calls Atatürk, in his own opinion, a very progressive statesman who aligned the new Turkey with Western principles.

> Der damals nach meiner Meinung sehr fortschrittliche Staatsmann Mustafa Kemal Atatürk hat auch die „sechs Pfeile“ des neuen türkischen Staatswesens eingeführt.
>
> [Translation:] The statesman Mustafa Kemal Atatürk, in my opinion a very progressive one for his time, also introduced the 'six arrows' of the new Turkish state.

**Source.** Bundesrat, 839th sitting, 12 March 2015, Stenographisches Protokoll p. 46 — <https://www.parlament.gv.at/dokument/BR/BRSITZ/839/fnameorig_411828.html>

**Caveat.** Used to argue that the secular founder banned the fez and the headscarf, against Turkey's present course; the praise serves a point about Islam in Austria. Backs the claim with: 'dass gerade Mustafa Kemal Atatürk sich sehr wohl total nach westlichen Prinzipien ausgerichtet hat'.

## Josef Cap — 2016-10-13

Criticising Turkey's re-Islamisation, says Atatürk would turn in his grave: he had really tried reforms and to bring Turkey closer to Europe's values.

> Kemal Atatürk im Grabe umdrehen würde. Er hat nämlich wirklich versucht, Reformen zu setzen und die Türkei stärker an Europa und an die europäischen Werte heranzuführen.
>
> [Translation:] Kemal Atatürk would turn in his grave. For he really did try to bring in reforms and to move Turkey closer to Europe and to European values.

**Source.** Nationalrat, 148th sitting of the XXV. GP, 13 October 2016, Stenographisches Protokoll p. 139-140 — <https://www.parlament.gv.at/dokument/XXV/NRSITZ/148/fnameorig_584143.html>

**Caveat.** Foreign-policy remarks after the July 2016 coup attempt, using Atatürk as the measure of Erdoğan's Turkey (instrumental as well as admiring). Speaker line: 'Abgeordneter Dr. Josef Cap (SPÖ)'.

## Robert Lugar — 2019-05-15

In the headscarf-ban debate presents Atatürk as neither a Muslim-hater nor right-wing, but a founder who banned the headscarf to end women's oppression.

> Das war kein Muslimhasser und das war auch kein Rechter. Der Gründer der Türkei hat das Kopftuch verboten, weil es eben genau mit der Unterdrückung der Frau zusammenhängt.
>
> [Translation:] That was no Muslim-hater and no right-winger either. The founder of Turkey banned the headscarf precisely because it is bound up with the oppression of women.

**Source.** Nationalrat, 74th sitting of the XXVI. GP, 15 May 2019, Stenographisches Protokoll p. 228 — <https://www.parlament.gv.at/dokument/XXVI/NRSITZ/74/fnameorig_779563.html>

**Caveat.** Argument for the 2019 ban on headscarves in primary schools; Atatürk is invoked as authority, so the record is as much instrumental as admiring. The historical claim (a headscarf ban) is the speaker's, not the vault's. Speaker line: 'Abgeordneter Ing. Robert Lugar (FPÖ)'.
