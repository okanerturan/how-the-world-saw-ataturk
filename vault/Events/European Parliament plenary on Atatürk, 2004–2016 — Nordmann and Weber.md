# European Parliament plenary on Atatürk, 2004–2016 — Nordmann and Weber

Five statements about Atatürk in the verbatim reports of the European Parliament. The EP's own pages sit behind a WAF that refuses scripted fetches, so each sitting was read by rendering its day report once. Turkey-accession and progress-report sittings read: 1 Apr 2004, 14-15 Dec 2004, 28 Sep 2005, 26 Sep 2006, 13 Dec 2006, 13 Feb 2007, 24 Oct 2007, 21 May 2008, 11 Mar 2009, 10 Feb 2010, 8 Mar 2011, 28 Mar 2012, 12 Jun 2013, 13 Apr 2016, 22 and 24 Nov 2016, 5-6 Jul 2017, 12 Mar 2019, 23 Oct 2019, 18 May 2021, 6 Jun 2022 and others. No statement was found by Verhofstadt, Cohn-Bendit, Schulz, Juncker, Farage, Le Pen or Lagendijk; Otto von Habsburg left the Parliament in 1999. MEPs who mention Atatürk without a view (attributions, wreaths, comparisons: Batten, Reid, Camre, Zaleski, Allam, Carver and others) are listed in the candidates file.

## Manfred Weber — 2016-11-22

After the coup attempt, recalls 'a great Turk', Atatürk, citing his sayings on peace and fear-free sovereignty as Turkey's own tradition.

> Ich möchte heute in meinem Redebeitrag an einen großen Türken erinneren, nämlich an Atatürk. Peace at home – peace in the world, war einer seiner Sätze, die er geprägt hat.
>
> [Translation:] In my speech today I would like to recall a great Turk, namely Atatürk. 'Peace at home - peace in the world' was one of the sayings he coined.

**Source.** European Parliament, Strasbourg, plenary 22 November 2016, 'EU-Turkey relations (debate)', verbatim report CRE-8-2016-11-22 item 10 — <https://www.europarl.europa.eu/doceo/document/CRE-8-2016-11-22-ITM-010_EN.html>

**Caveat.** Original German as in the verbatim report ('erinneren' is the report's misspelling). He goes on to quote Atatürk's line that sovereignty built on fear and arms alone cannot last, and to tell Ankara to argue from Turkey's own tradition rather than 'our European values'; the praise serves his message to Erdoğan's government, hence instrumental. Role: the report names him 'im Namen der PPE-Fraktion'; his CSU membership is the standard record.

## Jean-Thomas Nordmann — 2004-04-01

Defending Turkish secularism against the rapporteur, calls it perhaps the most valuable part of Kemal Atatürk's legacy and a recipe for the Union's future.

> Paragraph 32 of your report seems to me to proceed from a slightly jaundiced view of the secularism which is perhaps the most valuable part of the legacy of Kemal Atatürk.

**Source.** European Parliament, Strasbourg, plenary 1 April 2004, 'Progress towards accession by Turkey' (Oostlander report), verbatim report CRE-5-2004-04-01 item 2 — <https://www.europarl.europa.eu/doceo/document/CRE-5-2004-04-01-ITM-002_EN.html>

**Caveat.** One sentence of a short intervention on the Oostlander report. The report prints only 'Nordmann (ELDR)'; the first name is the standard record (M-tier).
