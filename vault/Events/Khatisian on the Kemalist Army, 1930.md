# Khatisian on the Kemalist Army, 1930

Alexander Khatisian (Khatisyan) was prime minister of the Republic of Armenia in 1919–20. His memoir, serialised in *Hairenik Monthly* and printed in Athens in 1930
(*The Origin and Development of the Republic of Armenia*), narrates the 1920 war with the Kemalist government
from the Armenian side. A 2025 English edition (translation and notes by Fatma Sarıkaya, edited by Mehmet
Arif Demirer, with editorial commentary in the footnotes) is online.

On the Kemalist movement the text is mostly narrative, with one stated assessment (PDF p. 141):
*"Turkey, under the leadership of Mustafa Kemal, started to establish a national army. Its aim was to protect
Turkey's interests. It must be admitted that the leaders of this movement have displayed a great deal of
energy and talent."* Elsewhere (edition p. ~162, the Armenian government's analysis of the autumn 1920 situation)
the Kemalists' aims are summarised as seeking Turkey's peace treaty through Russia and Azerbaijan and
"demolishing of the independent Armenia".

**Reading.** A victim-state premier grants the adversary talent while describing its aims as hostile to
Armenia. The vault records this as the speaker's account; the editors of the English edition add their own
notes, which are not the voice recorded here. Context: [[Armenian Genocide]], [[Treaty of Lausanne]].
