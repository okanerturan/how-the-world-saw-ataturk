# Greco-Turkish War

The Greek invasion of Anatolia, backed by [[David Lloyd George]] and driven by [[Eleftherios Venizelos]]'s *Megali Idea*. Broken at Dumlupınar, where [[Nikolaos Trikoupis]] was captured. Its
aftermath — the [[Burning of Smyrna]] and the [[Greco-Turkish Population Exchange]] — created
[[Aristotle Onassis]] the refugee and haunted [[Ernest Hemingway]] the young reporter.
