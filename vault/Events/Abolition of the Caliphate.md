# Abolition of the Caliphate

The single most consequential pen stroke in this vault. Ended a 1,300-year institution overnight and
gave [[Abdulmejid II]] twenty-four hours to leave the country.

Its blast radius: it devastated [[Ahmad Shawqi]] and [[Mehmet Akif Ersoy]], blindsided [[Mahatma Gandhi]] mid-[[Khilafat Movement]], triggered [[Sheikh Said Rebellion]], destroyed [[Hussein bin Ali]]'s dynasty, was pretexted on a letter from [[Aga Khan III]], delighted [[Periyar E. V. Ramasamy]] and [[B.R. Ambedkar]], appalled [[Allama Iqbal]], [[Haj Amin al-Husseini]] and [[Muammar Gaddafi]], and became the founding grievance of [[Sayyid Qutb]] and [[Ruhollah Khomeini]].
