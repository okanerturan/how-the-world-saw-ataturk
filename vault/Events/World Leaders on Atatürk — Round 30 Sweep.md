# World Leaders on Atatürk — Round 30 Sweep

Heads of state and government, ministers and party leaders found by the round-30 Monid sweep (Exa web search and SerpApi Google Books over 546 names not yet in the census) and verified at the source: official transcripts, presidency and UN sites, the press of record. Wreath-laying, protocol formulas and labels were rejected; each record below is the speaker's own stated view of Atatürk. Translations are marked.

## Vladimir Lenin, 1921
*Chairman of the Council of People's Commissars, Soviet Russia* — admiring, memoir, evidence P.

Briefing his envoy, calls Mustafa Kemal no socialist but a good organiser, talented commander and clever statesman leading a bourgeois-national revolution.

> — Конечно, Мустафа Кемаль-паша не социалист,— говорил Владимир Ильич,— но, по-видимому, хороший организатор, талантливый полководец, ведет буржуазно-национальную революцию, прогрессивно настроенный человек, умный государственный деятель. [English translation: 'Of course, Mustafa Kemal Pasha is not a socialist,' Vladimir Ilyich said, 'but he is evidently a good organiser, a talented commander, leading a bourgeois-national revolution, a progressive-minded man, a clever statesman.']

Mediated by: S. I. Aralov, Vospominaniya sovetskogo diplomata 1922–1923 (Moscow: IMO, 1960), pp. 34–35, recalling his briefing by Lenin.

⚠️ Recollected about 38 years later in a Soviet-era memoir; Lenin's words are Aralov's reconstruction. The meeting took place in Moscow between Aralov's recall in early November 1921 and his departure for Ankara at the end of December 1921 (pp. 23, 33–36). The scan reads 'не социалист' as '—: е социалист' (OCR). Lenin goes on to say he is sure Kemal 'will knock the arrogance out of the imperialists' and that Russia must help the Turkish people. Read in the archive.org open text.

## Theodor Heuss, 1929-01
*German liberal publicist and Reichstag deputy, later first Federal President* — hostile, article, evidence S.

After a Balkan journey, judges Kemal's Turkey a 'parliamentary-clothed dictatorship' that arranges elections and holds power through police and courts.

> parlamentarisch umkleidete Diktatur

Mediated by: Mathie 2023, Der Türken- und Türkeidiskurs in Schulbüchern 1919–1945 (Klinkhardt, open access), quoting Heuss, 'Balkanreise', Die Hilfe 35.1 (1929), pp. 20–22, here p. 21.

⚠️ Only the phrase and the paraphrase ('Wahlen arrangiere', held up by 'Polizei und Gericht') are given in the secondary source; Heuss's article itself was not read. Mangold-Will (Begrenzte Freundschaft, 2013, p. 253 snippet) says Heuss's 1928 assessment of Turkey was positive overall despite this. A judgement of Kemal's regime rather than of the man; hence conflicted.

## Konrad Adenauer, 1954-03-18
*Chancellor of West Germany; reply to Prime Minister Menderes's dinner speech, Ankara Palas, on his official visit* — admiring, speech, evidence J.

The 'genius leader, the unforgettable Atatürk', who with the noble Turkish nation worked wonders out of a state sunk in endless difficulties and political hopelessness; the German nation's deep admiration.

> Müsaade buyurursanız, ben de Alman milletinin asil Türk milleti ve onun dahi önderi unutulmaz Atatürk'ün nice sonsuz zorluklar ve siyasi ümitsizlikler içinde bulunan bir devletten ne gibi harikalar yaratmağa muktedir olduğunu görmekle duyduğu derin hayranlığı ifade etmek isterim. [translation: If you will allow me, I too would like to express the deep admiration the German nation feels at seeing what wonders the noble Turkish nation and its genius leader, the unforgettable Atatürk, were able to create out of a state caught in endless difficulties and political hopelessness.]

Mediated by: Milliyet, 19 Mar 1954, p. 7 ('Almanya Başvekili dün Ankaraya geldi', section 'Adenauer şerefine verilen ziyafet'): the paper's Turkish rendering of a speech given in German.

⚠️ Opening of his reply to Menderes at the dinner on his arrival day; he goes on to Turkey as a factor of stability in the Near East and its place in the Western alliance. Earlier that day he laid a wreath at Anıtkabir and stood in silence (Milliyet p. 1, reporter's narration). The German original (Bulletin des Presse- und Informationsamtes der Bundesregierung, March 1954) was not seen. Read on the page image (Okan's Milliyet archive login).

## Josip Broz Tito, 1954-04-13
*President of Yugoslavia; reply toast at President Bayar's dinner, Çankaya, Ankara, during his state visit* — admiring, speech, evidence J.

Ankara, the Turkish seat of government, as the dignified expression of a national strength inspired by the memory of 'the legendary personality of the great Atatürk'.

> Türkiyenin hükûmet merkezinde bulunmaktan derin bir memnunluk duymaktayım. Bu Türk milletinin büyük Atatürkün efsanevî şahsiyeti hatırasından mülhem olan kuvvetinin ve canlı millî şuurunun vakur bir ifadesidir. [translation: I feel deep satisfaction at being in the seat of government of Turkey. It is the dignified expression of the Turkish nation's strength, inspired by the memory of the legendary personality of the great Atatürk, and of its living national consciousness.]

Mediated by: Milliyet, 14 Apr 1954, p. 7 ('Türk-Yugoslav siyasî görüşmeleri başladı', section 'Titonun nutku'): the paper's Turkish rendering of a toast given in Serbo-Croatian.

⚠️ Opening lines of the reply toast; the rest is about Yugoslav sympathy, the Ankara Pact and Balkan peace. The same day he laid a wreath at Anıtkabir (Milliyet p. 1); no book entry is printed. The original Serbo-Croatian text (Borba / Politika, 14 Apr 1954; Govori i članci vol. IX) was not seen. The Instagram line 'Sizi hayatım boyunca daima ilham kaynağı olarak yaşattım' attributed to his Anıtkabir entry has no source. Read on the page image (Okan's Milliyet archive login).

## Theodor Heuss, 1957-05-06
*Federal President of West Germany* — admiring, speech, evidence S.

On the first German state visit since the Kaiser, likens post-war Germany to Turkey after 1918, which began a new chapter through Atatürk's great statesmanship.

> Wir werden mit ihnen fertig werden, wie die Türkei nach 1918 mit den Lasten einer Vergangenheit fertig wurde und in dem großen Staatsmanntum von Kemal Atatürk ein ganz neues Blatt der Staatsgeschichte zu schreiben begonnen hat. [English translation: We will cope with them, as Turkey after 1918 coped with the burdens of a past and, in the great statesmanship of Kemal Atatürk, began to write a wholly new page of its state history.]

Mediated by: Mathie 2023 (open access), quoting Heuss, 'Erwiderung des Herrn Bundespräsidenten auf die Ansprache des Türkischen Staatspräsidenten bei dem Abendessen am 6. Mai ds. Js. in Ankara', 1957, Archiv der Stiftung Bundespräsident-Theodor-Heuss-Haus, B122, 924.

⚠️ Reply to President Bayar's toast at the state dinner, Ankara, 6 May 1957; 'ihnen' = the consequences of the war (bracketed in the source). Read in the archive.org open text of the book's introduction; the archive typescript itself was not seen.

## Alija Izetbegović, 1970
*Bosnian Muslim activist, later first President of the Presidency of Bosnia and Herzegovina* — hostile, book, evidence P+J.

Says Kemal was more commander than cultural reformer, his merits must be cut down, and his reforms made Turkey a third-rate European copy.

> Tako na primjer, Mustafa Kemal, koji je očigledno bio veći vojskovođa nego kulturni reformator i čije zasluge za Tursku treba svesti na pravu mjeru, jednom od svojih reformi zabranjuje nošenje fesa. [English translation: Thus, for example, Mustafa Kemal, who was obviously a greater military leader than a cultural reformer and whose merits for Turkey should be reduced to their true measure, bans the wearing of the fez in one of his reforms.]

⚠️ Islamska deklaracija, written 1970, circulated in samizdat and published in Sarajevo in 1990; it was the main exhibit in the 1983 Sarajevo trial. Read in a full-text PDF of the work hosted by a site hostile to its author (n-um.com), p. 5; the same sentences are quoted by DEPO Portal/Prometej.ba (2017) and the Preporod yearbook. The text also calls Turkey 'as a European plagiarism' a third-rate country and says abolishing the Arabic script dragged Turkey 'to the borders of barbarism'. A later SDA line ('Atatürk was mostly a great military commander', Google Books snippet) is not staged.

## Ahmed Ben Bella, 1984-07
*First President of Algeria (in exile)* — conflicted, quote, evidence J.

Calls Mustafa Kemal extraordinary for defying the Greeks and Western powers and admires him, but says treating Turks as Western was a vital mistake.

> Türkleri Batılı sayması, çok hayati bir hataydı. Fakat bu bir yana, benim kendisine büyük hayranlığım var. [English translation: His counting the Turks as Western was a very vital mistake. But that aside, I have great admiration for him.]

Mediated by: Cumhuriyet, 16 Jul 1984 p. 8, series 'Ben Bella anlatıyor' (Geneva interview, printed in Turkish translation).

⚠️ Q&A interview printed in the newspaper (the 15 July instalment is bylined Cengiz Çandar), read in the Cumhuriyet e-archive OCR text of the page (OCR slips corrected: 'hayranhğım'). He also says Mustafa Kemal became 'olağanüstü bir insan' by resisting the Greeks and the Western powers behind them when Turkey was to be wiped off the map, that he admires his military personality, and, after 'Ancak' (however), that they do not share his later remedies (OCR 'getirdiği çarelerle ayru fikirde değiliz', read as 'aynı fikirde değiliz'). Says Algerian activists studied Kemal in the 1930s-40s.

## Rajiv Gandhi, 1988-07-08
*Prime Minister of India* — admiring, quote, evidence P.

Asked his view, says India greatly admires Atatürk as a great nationalist who laid modern Turkey's foundations, esteemed for democracy, secularism and modernisation.

> We have great admiration for Kemal Ataturk. Ataturk was a great nationalist who laid the foundations of modern Turkey.

⚠️ Joint interview with İlnur Çevik (Turkish Daily News) and Hasan Cemal (Cumhuriyet), New Delhi, 8 July 1988, before his Turkey visit; printed in Rajiv Gandhi: Selected Speeches and Writings, vol. 4 (1988), pp. 490–491 (Publications Division; archive.org open text). The question cites Nehru's admiration for Atatürk.

## Rajiv Gandhi, 1988-07-17
*Prime Minister of India* — admiring, speech, evidence P.

In Ankara, calls Atatürk the shining example Nehru held up for India's freedom struggle and his secular Republic a remarkable, inspiring example.

> Kemal Ataturk was the shining example whom Jawaharlal Nehru often placed before himself and his people in describing the vision of India which inspired us to struggle for our freedom.

⚠️ Speech at the banquet hosted by Prime Minister Turgut Özal, Ankara, 17 July 1988; Selected Speeches and Writings, vol. 4, pp. 361–364 (archive.org open text). He quotes Nehru's 1928 Calcutta praise of 'Kemal Pasha' and Atatürk's line that the Republic 'can never be the land of sheikhs, dervishes', then says: 'The achievements of Ataturk's Republic of Culture remain a remarkable, inspiring example.' Also: 'Turkey under Ataturk became a byword for modernisation.' Diplomatic banquet speech. The Turkish rendering 'Kendi özgürlük mücadelemizde Kemal Atatürk'ten ilham aldık' circulating in Turkish books matches this passage in sense.

## Fidel Castro, 1997-03-13
*President of Cuba; audience for the Turkish health minister's delegation, Palace of the Revolution, Havana* — admiring, meeting, evidence P+J.

A great man and a great soldier who somehow beat the British, a soldier and a thinker who entered history; told that Atatürk changed the Turkish script, Castro said he himself could not have made such a change in the order of things.

> "Atatürk büyük insan, büyük asker İngilizleri nasıl yendi?" durakladı... tekrar; "onları nasıl yendi?" dedi. "Asker ve fikir adamı Atatürk önemli insan olarak tarihe geçti." [translation: 'Atatürk, a great man, a great soldier — how did he beat the British?' He paused… again: 'How did he beat them?' 'Atatürk, soldier and man of ideas, has gone down in history as an important person.']

Mediated by: Mehmet Domaç (president of the Turkish Pharmacists' Association), notes taken during the audience in a notebook Castro signed, from the Spanish interpreter Mehmet Necati Kutlu's renderings; printed in TEB Haberler, May–June 1997, p. 8.

⚠️ Audience of 13 Mar 1997, 22:00–23:50, for Health Minister Yıldırım Aktuna's delegation, which handed him a letter from President Demirel. On the alphabet reform Domaç records only: 'Ben [böyle bir] düzen değişikliği yapamazdım' ('I could not have made such a change of order'). The wider line 'I made such a great revolution but could not achieve what Atatürk did' rests on Aktuna's account to the press after the meeting (Sabah, 15 Mar 1997; Milliyet the same day, 'Castro, irticayı sordu'), not on Domaç's notes. The official delegation report printed in the TBMM record (Term 20, vol. 35, 10th sitting, 5 Nov 1997, p. 89) confirms that his questions and remarks on Atatürk's person, the War of Independence and the reforms 'greatly impressed the Turkish delegation'. Later wordings ('a star that fell on your country', 'the only leader who governed his country even after his death') come from Vecdet Öz's social-media posts of 2020–2023 and are not in any 1997 record. Turkish in all three sources; translation by the census.

## Islam Karimov, 2000-10-17
*President of Uzbekistan* — admiring, quote, evidence J+J.

Tells Turkish journalists his path is Atatürk's, that he took Atatürk as his model, and that Atatürk's enemies in Turkey are his enemies.

> Kim ki Türkiye'de Atatürk'ün düşmanıdır; o kişi benim de düşmanımdır. Kim ki Atatürk'ü sever; o benim de dostumdur. Benim yolum Atatürk'ün yoludur. Kendime Atatürk'ü model aldım. [English translation: Whoever is Atatürk's enemy in Turkey is my enemy too. Whoever loves Atatürk is my friend. My path is Atatürk's path. I took Atatürk as my model.]

Mediated by: Hürriyet, 18 Oct 2000, and Cumhuriyet, 18 Oct 2000 p. 10 (Turkish press renderings).

⚠️ Said in Tashkent during President Sezer's visit, at the Timurid History Museum, the day Sezer left for Turkmenistan. Cumhuriyet's wording differs: 'Büyük Kemal Atatürk'ün yolu bizim için makbul yol... Türkiye'de kim ki Kemal Atatürk'e karşı çıkarsa, o bana karşı çıkmış demektir.' In the same remarks he praises the Turkish General Staff's fight against 'irtica' and complains of Uzbek Islamist exiles in Istanbul; his secular authoritarianism frames the praise. A 1998 statement ('follows Ataturk's precepts', Jamestown Monitor 14 Apr 1998, agencies) is single-source and not staged.

## Carl Bildt, 2004-09-28
*Former Prime Minister of Sweden (later Foreign Minister)* — admiring, speech, evidence P+J.

Calls Kemal Atatürk undoubtedly the most significant European revolutionary of the last century, who swept the old order away, sometimes by rather authoritarian methods.

> Kemal Ataturk is undoubtedly the most significant European revolutionary of the last century.

⚠️ Speech 'The Turks – and a New Europe – are coming!', SEB conference, Istanbul, 28 Sep 2004, text on Bildt's own site (bildt.net cb000089.pdf), read in full. Next lines: 'The old order was swept away – sometimes with rather authoritarian methods.' He casts Atatürk, Özal and Erdoğan as three phases of reform in an argument for Turkey's EU accession. Quoted by the International Herald Tribune (11 Dec 2006) when he became foreign minister.

## Kostas Karamanlis, 2008-01-24
*Prime Minister of Greece; entry in the Anıtkabir special book* — instrumental, quote, evidence J+J.

On the first Greek premier's official visit since 1959, says Atatürk and Venizelos had the courage and vision not to let past tragedies block peace.

> Kemal Ataturk and [then Greek leader] Eleftherios Venizelos had the political courage, will and vision not to allow the conflicts and tragedies of the past to become an obstacle to… building a better future of peace and co-operation to the benefit of the two peoples.

Mediated by: Cyprus Mail, 25 Jan 2008 (Reuters, English, with its own ellipsis and bracket); Hürriyet, 24 Jan 2008 (Turkish rendering).

⚠️ Entry in the Anıtkabir special book; Anıtkabir visits are a taboo in Greece and he did not tour the museum celebrating the 1922 victory over Greece. Hürriyet's Turkish: 'Atatürk ve Venizelos'un siyasi irade ve cesareti vardı. Biz de geçmiş trajedilerin bize engel olmaması için gerekli cesarete sahibiz.' Praise framed for Greek–Turkish rapprochement. Check: the quote is Reuters' English rendering; Hürriyet's Turkish differs; no original-language entry found.

## Dmitry Medvedev, 2010-05-10
*President of Russia* — admiring, article, evidence P+J.

Writing for Turkish readers before his visit, says he deeply respects Atatürk as an outstanding reformer whose contribution to new Turkey cannot be overestimated.

> У меня вызывает глубокое уважение личность Мустафы Кемаля Ататюрка – выдающегося реформатора, вклад которого в создание новой Турции трудно переоценить. [English translation (Kremlin): I feel great respect for Mustafa Kemal Ataturk, an outstanding reformer whose contribution to building modern Turkey cannot be overestimated.]

⚠️ Article 'Россия – Турция: не останавливаясь на достигнутом' for the Turkish daily Zaman, published on kremlin.ru on 10 May 2010 (English version dated 9 May). The next sentence turns to Russia as the first country to recognise the young Republic and its aid in the independence war. His Anıtkabir book entry on 12 May (kremlin.ru news 7720) adds that Atatürk 'helped to develop all-round relations with Russia'.

## Rafael Correa, 2012-03-15
*President of Ecuador* — admiring, quote, evidence J+J.

Says the Arab Spring countries would not be in their present plight had they had an Atatürk and made their political transformation in time.

> Turkey has become a model country in the Arab Spring. If those countries had had an Atatürk and accomplished their political transformations at the time, they would not have fallen into their current situation.

Mediated by: Hürriyet Daily News, 15 Mar 2012 (English rendering of remarks made through an interpreter); Turkish agency text reproduced on gzpolat2.blogspot.com.

⚠️ Joint press conference with President Abdullah Gül, Çankaya, 15 March 2012. The Spanish original was not found; the official Gül archive (abdullahgul.gen.tr, 82370) prints only Gül's own words. Turkish wording: 'Eminim ki bu ülkelerin de zamanında bir Atatürk'ü olmuş olsaydı ve siyasi değişimlerini o zaman yapabilselerdi, şu anda yaşadıkları durumlara düşmezlerdi.' Two renderings of one interpreted remark, so independent only as reports. Check: the quote is the interpreter's English as printed by Hürriyet Daily News; no Spanish original found.

## Paul Kagame, 2012-03-23
*President of Rwanda* — admiring, speech, evidence P.

Says modern Turkey knows the value of long-term visionary leadership, having had such leaders going back to Mustafa Kemal Atatürk, with visible results.

> The people of modern Turkey know this very well because you have had such visionary leaders going back to Mustafa Kemal Ataturk – and the results in your country show this.

⚠️ Acceptance speech for an honorary doctorate from Fatih University, Istanbul, 23 Mar 2012, text on paulkagame.rw. The preceding sentence says leaders need 'long-term vision' and the ability to motivate their people; the next turns to Rwanda. Fatih University was later closed by decree after 2016 as Gülen-linked.

## Mariano Rajoy, 2014-02-11
*Prime Minister of Spain; entry in the Anıtkabir special book* — admiring, quote, evidence J+J.

Expresses his and the Spanish people's admiration and hopes Atatürk's example of achieving a united, modern Turkey serves Spain as a reference.

> Que su ejemplo por haber logrado una Turquía unida y moderna nos sirva de referencia para saber que, con determinación, trabajo y tesón, es posible superar todo tipo de dificultades.

Mediated by: EFE (via Antena 3, 11 Feb 2014) and La Razón, 12 Feb 2014.

⚠️ Entry in the Anıtkabir book of honour on the first day of the Spain–Turkey high-level meeting. EFE and La Razón quote the same sentence (La Razón omits one comma) and both say he wrote of his 'admiración' and that of the Spanish people. Habertürk (12 Feb 2014) reports Spanish press reading it against Catalan and Basque separatism; that reading is the press's, not Rajoy's.

## Muhammadu Buhari, 2017-10-19
*President of Nigeria; entry in the Anıtkabir special book* — admiring, quote, evidence P+J.

Calls Atatürk a great patriotic, visionary and courageous leader who shaped modern Turkey, and pays him profound respect.

> It is an honour and privilege to visit Anitkabir, the mausoleum of a great patriotic, visionary and courageous leader who shaped modern Turkey, during my official working visit to Ankara and the ninth D-8 summit in Istanbul.

Mediated by: Nigerian State House press release by Garba Shehu, 19 Oct 2017.

⚠️ Entry in the Anıtkabir book of honour, as released by the presidency; the rest of the entry is about Nigeria–Turkey relations. Also reported by Punch and The Sun (Nigeria) from the same release.

## Prabowo Subianto, 2025-04-10
*President of Indonesia* — admiring, speech, evidence P+J.

Tells the Turkish parliament Atatürk was his youthful hero, keeps his statue at office and home, and calls him the Global South's icon of courage.

> Kalau Saudara datang ke kantor saya di Jakarta, kalau saudara datang ke rumah saya di Jakarta, ada patung Mustafa Kemal Atatürk di kantor saya, di rumah saya. Bagi kami, tidak hanya di Indonesia saya bicara, di Global South, di negara-negara yang sedang berkembang, Mustafa Kemal Atatürk adalah sebuah ikon, sebuah contoh keberanian, contoh kepemimpinan, contoh patriotisme, contoh semangat tidak mengenal menyerah. [English translation: If you come to my office in Jakarta, if you come to my house in Jakarta, there is a statue of Mustafa Kemal Atatürk in my office, in my house. For us, and I speak not only of Indonesia but of the Global South, the developing countries, Mustafa Kemal Atatürk is an icon, an example of courage, of leadership, of patriotism, of a never-surrender spirit.]

⚠️ Official Indonesian transcript, Sekretariat Negara (setneg.go.id), 'Pidato Kenegaraan Presiden di Hadapan Parlemen Turki', 10 April 2025, read in full. Same passage also says 'sebagai anak muda pahlawan saya, ikon saya adalah Mustafa Kemal Atatürk dan Mehmed Sang Penakluk'; he pairs Atatürk with Mehmed II and calls Türkiye the continuation of the Ottomans. AA English renders it 'My hero, my icon was Mustafa Kemal Ataturk along with Ottoman Sultan Mehmed II'. BBC Indonesia (16 Apr 2025) reports a social-media controversy over praising Atatürk before an AKP-majority parliament.

## Anwar Ibrahim, 2026-01-08
*Prime Minister of Malaysia* — conflicted, quote, evidence J+J.

Says he disagrees with Atatürk on Islam, language and script, but this cannot erase his role in freeing Turkey.

> Tentunya, saya juga ada pendapat peribadi yang kuat, kerana tatkala dia memimpin dan menguasai, ada beberapa keputusan yang saya rasa tak serasi dengan pandangan saya, tentang Islam, pertukaran bahasa, tulisan dan sebagainya. …tetapi itu tidak boleh memadamkan sumbangan, peranan dan sikap rakyat Turkiye tentang seorang tokoh besar yang berjaya membebaskan negara mereka daripada percubaan meleburkan dan menjadikannya sebahagian daripada wilayah di bawah penjajah Eropah. [English translation: Of course I too have strong personal views, because when he led and ruled there were some decisions I feel are not compatible with my views, on Islam, the change of language, script and so on. …but that cannot erase the contribution, the role and the Turkish people's attitude towards a great figure who succeeded in freeing their country from attempts to dissolve it and make it part of territory under European colonisers.]

Mediated by: Bernama, 8 Jan 2026; Free Malaysia Today, 9 Jan 2026 (English).

⚠️ Press conference in Istanbul at the end of a three-day official visit, after photos of his wreath at Anıtkabir drew criticism in Malaysia. FMT's English: 'While I may personally have differing views on certain reforms implemented during his leadership, such as language and religious policies, this does not diminish his role.' He says the ceremony is required protocol for every visiting leader, as for Mahathir and Najib before him.

## António Guterres, 2026-03-12
*UN Secretary-General* — admiring, speech, evidence P.

Accepting the Atatürk Peace Award, says Atatürk understood peace as a deliberate choice and his 'peace at home' motto captures the UN Charter's spirit.

> Through hardship and leadership, Atatürk understood that peace does not emerge by chance. It is a deliberate choice – anchored in social cohesion and sustained through international cooperation.

⚠️ Official text of the Secretary-General's remarks upon receiving the 2025 Atatürk International Peace Award from President Erdoğan, Presidential Complex, Ankara, 12 March 2026 (UN in Türkiye). He adds that Atatürk's 'commitment to social progress, equality, and the welfare of all resonates deeply with the mission of the United Nations'. Award-acceptance speech; he visited Anıtkabir the same day. Check: the cited UN pages do not state the award year or an Anıtkabir visit that day; treat those details as unsourced.
