# Indian Legislative Assembly Mourns Kemal, 14 November 1938 — Desai, Nairang, Aney, Aikman, Zafar Ali Khan and Sir Abdur Rahim

At the start of business on 14 November 1938 the Leader of the Opposition, Bhulabhai Desai, asked the President of the Central Legislative Assembly (Delhi) to let the House record its loss at Kemal's death; five more voices followed and the President himself spoke warmly before adjourning the House as a mark of respect. The Muslim League asked for the adjournment; Nripendra Sircar, for the Government, did not object. Broader Indian reaction: [[India Mourns, November 1938]].

## Bhulabhai Desai (1938-11-14)

> He was, according to our humble appreciation, one of the most courageous of world reformers. In a day he abolished the purdah, in a day he established the equality of man and woman

Legislative Assembly Debates, vol. VII (Government of India Press, Delhi 1938), 14 Nov 1938, p. 2954. Desai asks that sympathy be conveyed to Ismet Inonu; he calls Kemal's 'courage' a legacy 'particularly applicable' to India. ## Ghulam Bhik Nairang (1938-11-14)

> It was given to Mustafa Kemal Pasha to effect such marvellous changes as to make him a veritable Messiah of modern Turkey

Legislative Assembly Debates, vol. VII (Government of India Press, Delhi 1938), 14 Nov 1938, pp. 2954-55. He also asks that the House adjourn for the day in respect. Text read from the OCR of the official report; obvious OCR slips are silently corrected in the quote.

## M. S. Aney (1938-11-14)

> In him the world has lost a towering personality. He belonged to that race of great men who had the rare good fortune and the rare gift of bringing back to life the dead

Legislative Assembly Debates, vol. VII (Government of India Press, Delhi 1938), 14 Nov 1938, p. 2955. He quotes a Sanskrit maxim that success depends on the mettle of the man. Text read from the OCR of the official report; obvious OCR slips are silently corrected in the quote.

## A. Aikman (1938-11-14)

> in the passing of Kemal Pasha there has been lost to civilization an outstanding figure, a soldier, a diplomat and a nation builder

Legislative Assembly Debates, vol. VII (Government of India Press, Delhi 1938), 14 Nov 1938, pp. 2955-56. Initials as printed; full name not given in the report. Text read from the OCR of the official report; obvious OCR slips are silently corrected in the quote.

## Zafar Ali Khan (1938-11-14)

> Mustafa Kemal Pasha's death means more to the Mussalman world than to any other section of humanity.

Legislative Assembly Debates, vol. VII (Government of India Press, Delhi 1938), 14 Nov 1938, pp. 2956-57. The Urdu verses are printed in transliteration in the report; they are not quoted here. He threatens that the Muslim League will walk out unless the House adjourns. Text read from the OCR of the official report; obvious OCR slips are silently corrected in the quote.

## Sir Abdur Rahim (1938-11-14)

> Kemal Ataturk not only saved Turkey from political annihilation by his military genius, but as a far-seeing statesman he laid a new foundation, a very strong foundation for the national life of his people

Legislative Assembly Debates, vol. VII (Government of India Press, Delhi 1938), 14 Nov 1938, p. 2957. He adjourned the House as a mark of respect once Nripendra Sircar and Desai agreed, and promised to convey sympathy to Ismet Inonu.
