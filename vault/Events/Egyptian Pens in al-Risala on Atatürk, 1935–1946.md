# Egyptian Pens in al-Risala on Atatürk, 1935–1946

Ten pieces from the Cairo weekly *al-Risala* (founded 1933 by Ahmad Hasan al-Zayyat), read from the Arabic Wikisource transcription of each issue and quoted at one or two sentences. The weekly's writers are the Egyptian and Arab literary establishment of the 1930s-40s; several are hostile over the caliphate, the Latin alphabet or Hagia Sophia, a few admire the soldier-statesman. Religion and the caliphate are recorded in each author's own framing, not the vault's. See also [[Ahmad Hasan al-Zayyat]], [[Four Poems on Atatürk — Melbourne, Damascus, Cairo and Hyderabad, 1933–1939]], [[Abolition of the Caliphate]], [[Hat Law]].

## Abd al-Wahhab Azzam — *al-Risala*, no. 104, 1 Jul 1935

*In a serial against 'the Kemalists', says Muslims counted the Ghazi their undisputed hero, yet he led the Kemalists' break with Islam.*

> وكان أشدهم إفراطاً في ذلك الغازي مصطفى كمال باشا الذي كان المسلمون جميعهم يعدونه بطل أبطالهم غير مدافع.
> (translation: The most extreme of them in that was the Ghazi Mustafa Kemal Pasha, whom all Muslims used to count the hero of their heroes, beyond dispute.)

Wikisource page: مجلة الرسالة/العدد 104/النهضة التركية الأخيرة. See [[Abd al-Wahhab Azzam]].

## Ahmad Hasan al-Zayyat — *al-Risala*, no. 88, 11 Mar 1935

*Addresses the great Ghazi Atatürk: praises him for reviving the Sick Man, then says he wronged Turkey's history against nature and logic.*

> ولكنك ظلمت تاريخك الخاص بمخالفة الطبيعة في التجديد، ومجانبة المنطق في الاصلاح.
> (translation: But you wronged your own history by going against nature in renewal and shunning logic in reform.)

Wikisource page: مجلة الرسالة/العدد 88/إلى أين يساق الأتراك؟. See [[Ahmad Hasan al-Zayyat]].

## Mustafa Sadiq al-Rafi'i — *al-Risala*, no. 106, 15 Jul 1935

*Through a fictional woman interlocutor: Mustafa Kemal is a rebel, not a reformer, a commander made vain by victory, whose hatred of religion proves it.*

> وليس الرجل مصلحاً ألبتة، بل هو قائد زهاه النصر الذي أتفق له
> (translation: The man is not a reformer at all, but a commander made vain by the victory that fell to him.)

Wikisource page: مجلة الرسالة/العدد 106/فلسفة الطائشة. See [[Mustafa Sadiq al-Rafi'i]].

## Ali al-Tantawi — *al-Risala*, no. 98, 20 May 1935

*On Hagia Sophia: the page of Islam was closed there 'in the name of this ... Atatürk', as it opened in Muhammad the Conqueror's name.*

> وشعروا أن أيا صوفيا قد ختمت فيها صفحة الإسلام، باسم هذا الـ. . (أتاتورك) كما فتحت باسم (محمد الفاتح)!
> (translation: And they felt that in Hagia Sophia the page of Islam had been closed, in the name of this ... (Atatürk), as it was opened in the name of (Muhammad the Conqueror)!)

Wikisource page: مجلة الرسالة/العدد 98/أيا صوفيا. See [[Ali al-Tantawi]].

## Abbas Mahmud al-Aqqad — *al-Risala*, no. 619, 14 May 1945

*Holds Mustafa Kemal up against Hitler: he spent a fraction of Hitler's armaments yet retook Istanbul with the Allied armies in it.*

> واستطاع مع ذلك أن يفتح الآستانة فتحاً ثانياً وفيها جيوش الحلفاء
> (translation: and yet he was able to conquer Istanbul a second time, with the Allied armies in it.)

Wikisource page: مجلة الرسالة/العدد 619/التربية السياسية. See [[Abbas Mahmud al-Aqqad]].

## Zaki Mubarak — *al-Risala*, no. 257, 6 Jun 1938

*Asks why no delegation goes to the Ghazi to remind him that grudge against the Arabs should not erase their gift of faith to Turkey.*

> هل فكرت في إرسال وفد إلى الغازي مصطفى كمال يذكره بأن الحقد على العرب الذين خذلوا تركيا في الحرب لا يصح أن ينسيه فضل العرب الأبرار الذين نقلوا إلى تركيا بذور الإيمان بالله والرسول؟
> (translation: Has it thought of sending a delegation to the Ghazi Mustafa Kemal to remind him that grudge against the Arabs who let Turkey down in the war must not make him forget the merit of the righteous Arabs who carried to Turkey the seeds of faith in God and the Prophet?)

Wikisource page: مجلة الرسالة/العدد 257/ليلى المريضة في العراق. See [[Zaki Mubarak]].

## Zaki Mubarak — *al-Risala*, no. 458, 13 Apr 1942

*Recalls that 'the late Mustafa Kemal' claimed Adam and Eve were Turks; he found the claim monstrous and wrote to Kemal that they were Arabs.*

> كان المرحوم مصطفى كمال زعم أن آدم وحواء من أصل تركيّ، وقد أزعجني هذا الزعم الفظيع
> (translation: The late Mustafa Kemal claimed that Adam and Eve were of Turkish origin, and this monstrous claim disturbed me.)

Wikisource page: مجلة الرسالة/العدد 458/اجتماع الملائكة في مسجد الفردوس. See [[Zaki Mubarak]].

## Muhammad Mandur — *al-Risala*, no. 586, 25 Sep 1944

*Cites Mustafa Kemal and Stalin as proof that determined will overcomes obstacles regardless of collective mind or social laws.*

> وأن إرادتنا لابد آتية على كافة الصعاب كما أتى مصطفى كمال على صعاب تركيا وستالين على صعاب روسيا
> (translation: and that our will must surely overcome all obstacles, as Mustafa Kemal overcame Turkey's obstacles and Stalin Russia's.)

Wikisource page: مجلة الرسالة/العدد 586/مكافحة الشكلية. See [[Muhammad Mandur]].

## Felix Faris — *al-Risala*, no. 257, 6 Jun 1938

*Calls Atatürk 'the shrewd' who knew the Turkish people's nature, a reformer who weakened every element out of tune with Turkey's spirit.*

> تلك حقائق لم تخف على الداهية أتاتورك فأنه عرف ما هي فطرة الشعب التركي وما هي الحالة الاجتماعية التي تتفق وما كمن في حوافزه.
> (translation: These facts were not hidden from the shrewd Atatürk, for he knew what the Turkish people's nature was and what social condition accorded with what lay hidden in its drives.)

Wikisource page: مجلة الرسالة/العدد 257/بين الشرق والغرب. See [[Felix Faris]].

## Ahmad Ramzi — *al-Risala*, no. 698, 18 Nov 1946

*Sees the Ghazi as one of the few in the East who acted after long thought; ends by predicting his 'bewildered soul' will be explained.*

> إذ هو من الأفراد القلائل في الشرق الذين عملوا بعد تفكير طويل، وأخضعوا أعمالهم لفكرتهم الأولى
> (translation: For he is one of the few individuals in the East who acted after long reflection and made their deeds obedient to their first idea.)

Wikisource page: مجلة الرسالة/العدد 698/الغازي مصطفى كمال. See [[Ahmad Ramzi]].
