# Oliver Baldwin on Kemal, 1932

A passing but approving remark in a British memoir of 1920s Anatolia. *Writing of Anatolian peasants, expects things to improve now that Kemal has limited the power of the priesthood.*

> Since Mustapha Kemal has limited the power of the priesthood, things will probably be better in the future,

[[Oliver Baldwin]]. Open scan (Digital Library of India copy of the 1932 book). A passing view inside a travelogue of 1920s Anatolia; the sentence is a prediction about the peasants. Other 'Kemal's in the book are his Circassian fellow-prisoner Yusuf Kemal, not Mustafa Kemal; the other Kemalist mentions (p. 165 the 17 September 1922 march; a later remark that Kemal's portrait hung in Indian Muslim shops) are descriptive. Sentence is cut off at the comma in the printed page; the text continues on p. 130. Cited as: Oliver Baldwin, The Questing Beast: An Autobiography (1932), p. 129 (and p. 130 for the Turks 'may become a very different race').
