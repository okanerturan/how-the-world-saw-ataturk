# Wikisource Pens on Mustafa Kemal — Lototsky, Iorga, Nzhdeh, Shant, Annadurai and Others

Texts in Wikisource editions in five languages (Ukrainian, Romanian, Armenian, Tamil, English), found by a keyword sweep of 30+ Wikisource languages for Kemal and Atatürk. Wikisource transcriptions, tier S; none of the linked scans was viewed. Kemal and Atatürk were not found in a usable named text in the Swedish, Danish, Norwegian, Dutch, Finnish, Estonian, Lithuanian, Hebrew and Georgian Wikisources (see the candidates file).

## Ukrainian — Lototsky (memoir, published 1966), Kotko (1930), Petriv (1924)

[[Oleksandr Lototsky]], the Ukrainian People's Republic's envoy in Constantinople, remembers Kemal as the gifted 'rebel general':

> Ставши на чолі визвольного руху, він здолав піднести національне почуття турецького народу до найвищого ступня.
> (translation: Having put himself at the head of the liberation movement, he managed to raise the national feeling of the Turkish people to its highest degree.)
> Той характер Кемалевого діла оновив психіку народу, оспалого за дотеперішніх політичних та культурних умовин, і подвигнув той народ до великого самосвідомого чину.
> (translation: That character of Kemal's work renewed the psyche of a people drowsy under the former political and cultural conditions and moved that people to a great self-conscious deed.)

[[Kost Kotko]], a Soviet Ukrainian travel writer, 1930:

> Молодотурки, завівши конституцію, нап'яли на неї, за одним фігуральним виразом, чалму. Чи напне на власну конституцію Кемаль капелюха всеевропейського капіталістичного зразка?
> (translation: The Young Turks, having introduced a constitution, put a turban on it, in one figurative expression. Will Kemal put on his own constitution a hat of the all-European capitalist pattern?)

[[Vsevolod Petriv]], a UNR general, 1924: 'the strong and mighty army of Kemal' as a model of an army that held together.

## Romanian — Nicolae Iorga, 31 October 1938

[[Nicolae Iorga]], opening lecture, University of Bucharest:

> Cînd s'a îmbolnăvit în urmă d. Atatürk, ce adînc sentiment de îngrijorare, de durere a fost în sufletul tuturor Turcilor: pare că ar fi crezut că dispariția aceluia care a restabilit gloria de odinioară a Imperiului, devenit național acum, ar însemna prăbușirea patriei și dispariția națiunii din rîndul popoarelor care înseamnă ceva în lume.
> (translation: When Mr Atatürk recently fell ill, what a deep feeling of worry and grief there was in the soul of all the Turks: it seems they believed that the disappearance of the man who restored the former glory of the Empire, now become national, would mean the collapse of the homeland and the disappearance of the nation from among the peoples that mean something in the world.)

## Armenian — Nzhdeh (1932, 1937) and Shant (1925)

Sensitive thread (Armenian genocide memory); each is the author's own polemic.

[[Garegin Nzhdeh]], 1932:

> երբ Քեմալը՝ բախտահաս պատահական յաղթութեամբ գինով, իր արնոտ ձեռքը ագռաւի թեւերին իջեցրած՝ Արաքսի ափէն իր պղտոր հայեացքով Արարատեան դաշտն է չափում, զայն մի օր սեւ հողերի վերածելու երազով.
> (translation: when Kemal, drunk with a lucky accidental victory, his bloody hand lowered on the raven's wings, measures the Ararat plain from the bank of the Araxes with his murky gaze, dreaming of one day turning it into black earth.)

Nzhdeh, 1937: *Bolshevism saved Turkey, and not the lucky Kemal.* (translation)

[[Levon Shant]], 1925:

> Այսինքն` հին թուրքեր եւ նոր թուրքեր, վերջիններս երէկ Իթթիհատական կը կոչուէին, այսօր քեմալական, վաղը նոր անունով մը պիտի իջնեն ասպարէզ, երբ խմբական նախանձը Քեմալին ալ տապալէ:
> (translation: That is, old Turks and new Turks; the latter were called Ittihadists yesterday, are Kemalists today, and tomorrow will enter the arena under a new name, when factional envy topples Kemal too.)

## Tamil — C. N. Annadurai, 1960

[[C. N. Annadurai]], letter 105 to his 'thambi':

> குவலயம் புகழப் பணியாற்றிய முஸ்தபா கமால், சமைத்தளித்த புதிய துருக்கியில் எழுச்சி, மறுமலர்ச்சி, விடுதலை உணர்ச்சி பூத்திடச் செய்திடும் ஆற்றலையும் பெற்றிருந்தவர்தான், மெண்டாரிஸ்.
> (translation: Menderes was one who also had the power to make revival, renaissance and the feeling of freedom bloom in the new Turkey that Mustafa Kamal, who had served to the praise of the whole world, had prepared and handed over.)

## English — McCabe (1945), Hook (1943), Banerjea (1925), Du Bois (1928)

[[Joseph McCabe]]: *He humanized and modernized Turkey with great vigor.* [[Sidney Hook]]: Atatürk among the 'midget followers of the pattern set by the dictators'. [[Surendranath Banerjea]]: the Khilafat settlement 'reinforced by the military triumphs of Kemal Pasha'. [[W.E.B. Du Bois]], in the novel *Dark Princess*, has a character say 'There I sat at the feet of Kemal and heard his plans' (fiction, a character's speech).
