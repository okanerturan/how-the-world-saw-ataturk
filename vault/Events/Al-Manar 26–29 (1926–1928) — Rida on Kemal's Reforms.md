# Al-Manar 26–29 (1926–1928) — Rida on Kemal's Reforms

Later volumes of *al-Manar* (IA [`Almanar`](https://archive.org/details/Almanar), `almanar26/28/29.pdf`), complementing the 1924 card. Unsigned editorial matter in Rida's own journal; I attribute it to Rida as editor and, where he writes "we" and cites his *Tafsir*, in his own voice.

**Vol. 26, part 7 (29 Jumada II 1344 / 14 Jan 1926), pp. 496-498 (pdf 382-384): the hat fatwa, headed "The Kemalists' flight from Islam".** Rida, answering a reader and a German writer, holds the hat permissible in itself but forbidden as a tool of the secularists: *"and the world will know the fate of the Turks from Mustafa Kemal's attempt to make them a new creation by these ways he has taken; we ask God to protect them from its evil outcome"* (وسيعلم العالم مصير الترك بمحاولة مصطفى كمال جعلهم خلقاً جديداً بهذه الطرق التي سلكها, p. 498). On p. 496 he says he believes Kemal "deliberately demolishes all the constituents of the Turkish people". *(English translation mine.)*

**Vol. 28, part 10 (30 Rajab 1346 / 23 Jan 1928), p. 785 (pdf 567), on the Afghan king's hat.** *"Sociologists of judgement and insight reckon that Mustafa Kemal's leap will be more dangerous to his people"* (يقدر أهل الرأي والبصيرة من علماء الاجتماع أن وثبة مصطفى كمال ستكون أشد خطراً على قومه): an old state shows such revival before it dies, like the flame of the wick before it goes out. *(English translation mine.)*

**Vol. 29, part 6 (30 Rabi II 1347 / 14 Oct 1928), pp. 456-470 (pdf 383-401): "The danger of the Kemalists' attack on Islam" (the Latin alphabet).** A first-person editorial. Section 3, "what Mustafa Kemal hopes to gain by his success", p. 466 (pdf 393), is the conflicted line: *"by my life, it is a great soul and a far-reaching ambition, though we Muslims abhor this new plan, and many of the wise think it unsound"* (ولعمري إنها لنفس كبيرة، وهمة بعيدة، وإن كنا نحن المسلمين نستنكر هذه الخطة الجديدة). He goes on to say Kemal aims to found a new nation and religion. *(English translation mine.)*

**Dry end.** Search of the OCR for vols. 30-35 (1929-35) found only Afghan-hat and passing items; not read. Vol. 27 p. 73 (pdf 68) compares Kemal to Mussolini but in a translated third-party article, so it is not recorded.
