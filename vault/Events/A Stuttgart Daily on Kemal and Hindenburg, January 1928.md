# A Stuttgart Daily on Kemal and Hindenburg, January 1928

A German review of Turkey's year 1927, found in the Deutsches Zeitungsportal by full-text search and read on the page image (18 Nov 2026).

**Stuttgarter Neues Tagblatt, 2 January 1928, evening edition, p. 1: "Das Jahr 1927 in der Türkei"**, by the paper's Constantinople correspondent (signed "d."). Most of it is satisfaction: some 500 km of new railway opened in 1927, and German firms building most of the power stations and factories. Then the complaint: Kemal had ignored the greetings on Hindenburg's birthday (his eightieth, 2 October 1927). "Ein Mann wie Kemal Pascha durfte den Glückwunsch zu Hindenburgs Geburtstag einfach nicht übersehen, zumal er selber sehr auf die Ehrung seiner Person hält, wie die Duldung der in diesem Jahre wie Pilze aus dem Boden geschossenen Kemaldenkmäler jedermann deutlich beweist." The new Turkey is young, the correspondent concludes; may its leaders learn international courtesy too. He still wishes the Turkish people "von Herzen Glück". Record: `stuttgarter-neues-tagblatt-1928-01-02`.

The article names no statue; the first public statues of him had gone up in 1926–1927, among them Sarayburnu in Istanbul and the Victory Monument in Ankara.
