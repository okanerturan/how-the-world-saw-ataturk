# Zafar Ali Khan — Angora, a poem in Baharistan (1937)

Poem 'Angora' in *Baharistan* parts 1-2 (Gujrat, 1937), printed p. 273, read on the page image; by [[Zafar Ali Khan]]. Twelve couplets, each hemistich ending 'Angora ka' (of Angora). Kemal is named in the opening line as the founder of the 'order of Angora'; the poet credits it with undoing Curzon's work, humbling Europe's pride, and says Afghanistan's Amanullah Khan is also intoxicated with it and foresees Kabul, Hind, Iraq, Egypt and Syria following its example.

Verbatim (Urdu): مصطفیٰ قائم نہ کرتا گر نظامِ انگورہ کا / کام کرزن نے کیا ہی تھا تمامِ انگورہ کا
Translation (marked): "Had Mustafa not established the order of Angora / Curzon had already finished off Angora."

Dating: undated in the book; probably 1922-24 (Curzon and Amanullah references), inferred, not read.
