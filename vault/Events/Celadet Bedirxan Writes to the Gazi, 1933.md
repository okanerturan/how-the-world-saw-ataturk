# Celadet Bedirxan Writes to the Gazi, 1933

After the general amnesty of 1933 (the republic's tenth anniversary), Celadet Alî Bedirxan, in exile in Damascus,
published an open letter in Ottoman-Turkish (Arabic script) addressed "Gazi Paşa Hazretleri". It appeared as
*Hawar Library* no. 6 (Damascus, 1933). He had been a founder of Xoybûn and a Bedirxan family exile
since the Lausanne period. The letter argues that the state cannot treat a "Kurdistan question" as
non-existent, and criticises the state's language policy toward Kurdish.

The passages below are quoted from a 2022 review that cites the 2010 Doz edition by page. I have not seen the
letter itself, so wording and pagination rest on that edition via the reviewer.

- On Kemal and the question (Doz ed. p. 39): *"Paşa Hazretleri, maruf olan şahsi ve medeni cesaretinize
  rağmen, bilmem ki neden şimdiye kadar Türkiye'de bir Kürdistan meselesinin mevcudiyetini sarahaten itiraf
  edemediniz ..."* ("Pasha, despite your well-known personal and civic courage, I do not know why until now
  you have not been able to admit openly that there is a Kurdistan question in Turkey", tr.). The same
  sentence goes on to say the question drives his government to "indecision, hesitation, retreats and
  half-measures".
- On language (p. 59): quotes a minister of education's remark that Kurdish is "the language of mountain
  Turks" and says the formula has become "fashionable" among governments that want to assimilate or destroy
  the Kurds.
- Publisher's blurb for the 2010 edition quotes the letter's request that the state recognise by official
  announcement "Kürdistan'ın mevcudiyetini, Kürtlerin tarihi, ırki, harsi haklarını".

**Reading.** Both courteous and accusatory: he grants Kemal courage and the unification of the two Turkeys
(Istanbul and Ankara) while charging him with evasion on the Kurdish question. Conflicted in form, hostile in
effect on policy. Context: [[Celadet Bedir Khan]], [[Alphabet Reform]], [[Ararat Rebellion]].
