# 2023 Atatürk Series Controversy

Proof the Rorschach is still live. For the Republic's centennial (29 October 2023), Disney+
commissioned a six-part series *Atatürk* starring Aras Bulut İynemli. In August 2023 the Armenian
National Committee of America campaigned against it as glorifying a perpetrator; Disney+ pulled
the series from its platform.

The Turkish reaction was sharp (the vault's characterisation): government condemnation (AKP spokesman Ömer
Çelik called it, in Al Jazeera's rendering, a "shame" and "disrespectful to the values of the Republic of Turkey
and our nation"; "shameful" is the wording of Yetkin's and TRT's reports, not a verbatim quotation), a broadcast-regulator investigation of Disney, and a wave of subscription
cancellations. The series was released anyway — a broadcast version on FOX TV Turkey and two
theatrical films.

Eighty-five years after the obituaries, the same contest over the same figure, running through a
streaming platform: Armenian-American memory of the [[Armenian Genocide]] era against Turkish
national memory, with an American entertainment corporation as the battlefield. The modern
counterpart to the November 1938 press corpus — see [[Death and State Funeral of Atatürk]] and
[[Can Dündar]] for the 2008 precursor.
