# Ministers, Premiers and Presidents in the Anıtkabir Special Book, 2009–2026

Round 15 found that Turkish news sites print what visiting foreign leaders write in the Anıtkabir special book (see [[Leaders in the Anıtkabir Special Book, 2004–2025]]). Round 16 adds
fifteen entries by heads of state, prime ministers, foreign ministers and one general, 2009–2026, again from Turkish wire copy (AA, DHA, İHA)
republished by several outlets. Every quote below was checked against the fetched page text. All are Turkish translations except
Kristersson's, which Middle East Monitor printed in English; no original-language text has been located. Where agencies disagree on the
Turkish wording (Obama, Tillerson) the note says so: the exact English is not fixed.

## What they chose to say

- **The US, four secretaries and a general:** [[Hillary Clinton]] (2009) pays respects to 'the founder of this great country'; [[John Kerry]] (2013) calls Atatürk 'a soldier and statesman who pioneered education and independence'; [[Rex Tillerson]] (2017) writes of 'deep respect and admiration' for 'the great and eminent leader'; [[Antony Blinken]] (2023) offers a courtesy line two weeks after the earthquakes; [[Dan Caine]] (2026) repeats the Tillerson formula almost word for word on behalf of the delegation, which suggests a protocol template. [[Barack Obama]] (2009) is the fullest: Atatürk's 'vision, tenacity and courage put the Republic of Turkey on the path of democracy' and his legacy 'continues to inspire the whole world'.
- **Europe:** [[François Hollande]] (2014) addresses 'the statesman who, after great suffering, built modern Turkey'; [[Matteo Renzi]] (2014) calls him 'founder of modern Turkey' and promises support on the EU road; [[Mark Rutte]] (2022) feels 'privileged' to return to honour his memory; [[Ulf Kristersson]] (2022) speaks of the Republic Atatürk founded and recalls photographs of him with the Swedish crown prince in 1934; [[Boris Johnson]] (2016) writes of returning to his ancestors' land and of the founder of 'modern, free and democratic Turkey'.
- **Kinship and the Turkic world:** [[Ilham Aliyev]] (2024) writes the longest and warmest entry, 'genius leader' and 'great son of the Turkic world'; [[Kassym-Jomart Tokayev]] (2022) sends respects to a people 'whose roots are one with ours'.
- **Peace and the Middle East:** [[Isaac Herzog]] (2022) calls Atatürk 'the great visionary' and speaks of the wisdom of his legacy of progress and peace, then adds a Hebrew verse from the Psalms.
- **Courtesy from Asia:** [[Lee Jae-myung]] (2025) writes 'commemorating Atatürk' and pledges common prosperity with the 'blood-brother' nation.

## How to read these

The book is signed on a protocol visit, so the lines are chosen by the visitor's staff for the host. Praise is cheap here; what is
telling is the angle each visitor picks: democracy (Obama), education (Kerry), the European road (Renzi), kinship (Aliyev, Tokayev),
family history (Johnson), peace (Herzog). Several entries name Atatürk only by the epithet 'founder' (Clinton, Hollande, Johnson), and
the quoted lines are the visitors' own choice of what to say before the tomb.



## Barack Obama (2009-04-06)

*Honoured to pay respects to Atatürk, whose vision, resolve and courage turned Turkey toward democracy and whose legacy inspires generations worldwide.*

> [translation] Vizyonu, kararlılığı ve cesaretiyle Türkiye Cumhuriyeti'ni demokrasiye yönelten ve mirası tüm dünyaya kuşaklar boyunca ilham vermeye devam eden Mustafa Kemal Atatürk'e saygılarımı sunmak, benim için bir onurdur.

Turkish rendering of an entry written in English. Habertürk and T24 print the same wording; Dengegazetesi (6 Apr 2009) gives a looser version ('Vizyonu, ısrarı ve cesareti, ...'), so the exact English is not fixed. The entry goes on to invoke 'Yurtta barış, dünyada barış' and to promise support for Atatürk's vision of Turkey as a modern, prosperous democracy. Separate artifact from the Assembly speech of the same day (record obama-2009). Evidence J. See [[Barack Obama]].


## Hillary Clinton (2009-03-07)

*Pays respects to the founder of 'this great country' and shows the friendship of the American people; says only that.*

> [translation] Bu büyük ülkenin kurucusuna saygımızı ve Amerikan halkının dostluğunu göstermek için ülkem adına burayı bir kez daha ziyaret etmekten onur duyuyorum.

Turkish rendering of an entry written in English; Habertürk, T24 and NTV print the same wording. Atatürk is not named in the quoted line: 'the founder of this great country' is the only reference, hence intensity 1. Habertürk and T24 are dated 7 Mar 2009, NTV 6 Mar 2009 (probably a pre-visit post). Evidence J. See [[Hillary Clinton]].


## John Kerry (2013-03-02)

*Pays deep respect to Atatürk as a soldier and statesman who pioneered education and independence.*

> [translation] Eğitim ve bağımsızlığın öncüsü bir asker ve devlet adamı olan Mustafa Kemal Atatürk'e derin saygılarımla. Türk halkına ve bu büyük lidere, Amerikan halkının bir temsilcisi olarak saygılarımı sunmaktan onur ve gurur duyuyorum.

Turkish rendering of an entry written in English. Hürriyet (2 Mar 2013, credited AA), Akşam and Cumhuriyet print the same Turkish text; all three are probably the same AA wire. Kerry flew out of Ankara after the visit. Evidence J. See [[John Kerry]].


## Rex Tillerson (2017-03-30)

*Calls Atatürk the great and eminent leader of the Republic and speaks of his deep respect and admiration.*

> [translation] Türkiye Cumhuriyeti'nin büyük ve üstün lideri Mustafa Kemal Atatürk'e olan derin saygı ve hayranlığımın bir göstergesi olarak, Amerikan halkının bir temsilcisi sıfatıyla, Türk halkına ve bu büyük lidere saygılarımı sunmaktan derin bir onur duyuyorum.

Turkish rendering of an entry written in English. The quote is the DHA wire as printed by Sözcü. Three agencies give three different Turkish renderings: AA ('büyük ve seçkin lideri ... derin saygı ve takdirle ... bu harika lidere'), İHA ('büyük lideri ... hayranlıklarımla') and DHA (as quoted), so the exact English wording cannot be fixed. Tillerson read the entry aloud (AA). The date 30 Mar 2017 is from the sondakika.com stamp of the DHA copy. Evidence J. See [[Rex Tillerson]].


## Antony Blinken (2023-02-20)

*Honoured to pay respects to the Turkish nation and to Atatürk; the entry is mostly about earthquake solidarity and the bond between the peoples.*

> [translation] Türk ulusu ve Büyük Önder Mustafa Kemal Atatürk'e saygılarımı sunmaktan büyük bir onur duyuyorum.

Turkish rendering of an entry written in English. Written two weeks after the Kahramanmaraş earthquakes; the rest of the entry speaks of the bond between the peoples 'since the birth of the Republic'. TGRT Haber and Şehitler Ölmez print it; whether both repeat one wire was not checked. Courtesy line with no evaluation of Atatürk. Evidence J. See [[Antony Blinken]].


## Isaac Herzog (2022-03-09)

*Calls Atatürk a great visionary and speaks of the wisdom of his legacy of progress and peace.*

> [translation] Büyük vizyoner Mustafa Kemal Atatürk`ü ebedileştiren bu tarihi mekânı ziyaret etmek farklı bir ayrıcalık.

Turkish rendering (AA) of an entry written partly in English and partly in Hebrew; the Hebrew line is a verse from the Psalms ('Depart from evil and do good; seek peace and pursue it'), reported as such by Cumhuriyet. The entry continues: following the wisdom of this great leader's legacy of progress and peace, 'let us sincerely embrace the many truths of the promise of a safer, more stable world'. Cumhuriyet is dated 10 Mar 2022; the visit date 9 Mar 2022 is taken from the Turkish Wikipedia visitor list, so check against an agency stamp. Cumhuriyet and Yurt Gazetesi are the same AA copy. Evidence J. See [[Isaac Herzog]].


## François Hollande (2014-01-27)

*Addresses the statesman who, after great suffering, built modern Turkey and revived friendship with France.*

> [translation] Büyük acılar sonrası modern Türkiye'yi kurmayı ve Fransa ile dostluğu tekrar canlandırmayı başaran devlet adamına

Turkish rendering of an entry written in English (French). The line is an address ('to the statesman who ...') and does not name Atatürk in the printed text; the context is the first state visit by a French president in 22 years. AA, Hürriyet, Sözcü, Dünya, Sondakika and Patronlar Dünyası print the same sentence (one AA wire). Evidence J. See [[François Hollande]].


## Matteo Renzi (2014-12-11)

*Pays deep respect to Atatürk as founder of modern Turkey and promises support for Turkey's path toward the European Union.*

> [translation] Modern Türkiye'nin kurucusu Mustafa Kemal Atatürk'e derin saygılarımı sunuyorum.

Turkish rendering of an entry written in English (Italian). Haberler (AA) and Haber7 print the same text, one AA wire. The sentences that follow concern Italian-Turkish relations and EU accession. Evidence J. See [[Matteo Renzi]].


## Mark Rutte (2022-03-22)

*Feels privileged to return to Anıtkabir to honour Atatürk's memory; the rest of the entry is about Dutch-Turkish ties.*

> [translation] Mustafa Kemal Atatürk'ün hatırasını onurlandırmak için yeni bir fırsatla Anıtkabir'e geri geldiğim için ayrıcalıklı hissediyorum.

Turkish rendering of an entry written in English (English). TürkInfo.nl and İktibas Dergisi print the same AA text. 'Geri geldiğim' means he had been there before. As NATO Secretary-General Rutte visited Anıtkabir again on 25 Nov 2024 (Akşam, AA), but no entry text was found. Evidence J. See [[Mark Rutte]].


## Kassym-Jomart Tokayev (2022-05-10)

*Remembers Atatürk, founder of the Turkish Republic, with mercy and sends respects to the Turkish people 'whose roots are one with ours'.*

> [translation] Türkiye Cumhuriyeti kurucusu Mustafa Kemal Atatürk'ü rahmetle anıyor, köklerimiz bir Türk halkına saygılarımı iletiyorum.

Turkish rendering (AA) of an entry probably written in Kazakh or Russian; the original is not located. Haberler (AA) and TRT Haber print the same text, one AA wire. The remainder wishes lasting friendship and cooperation. Evidence J. See [[Kassym-Jomart Tokayev]].


## Ilham Aliyev (2024-02-19)

*Calls Atatürk the great son of the Turkic world, a gifted commander and a genius leader who inscribed the Republic in world history.*

> [translation] Türk dünyasının büyük evladı, yetenekli komutan ve dahi lider Gazi Mustafa Kemal Atatürk'ün aziz hatırasını en içten saygı ve ihtiramla anıyorum.

Turkish rendering (AA) of an entry probably written in Azerbaijani; the original is not located. AA and Kıbrıs Gazetesi print it, one AA wire (the second has a typo, 'edebi' for 'ebedi'). The entry goes on to say that Atatürk 'knew Azerbaijan's joy as his own joy and its grief as his own grief'. Aliyev signed the book on earlier visits (5 Nov 2008, 12 Nov 2013, 15 Jan 2015, 25 Apr 2018 per the Turkish Wikipedia list); those entries were not found. Evidence J. See [[Ilham Aliyev]].


## Boris Johnson (2016-09-27)

*Calls it a privilege to return to his ancestors' land and honour the founder of modern, free, democratic Turkey, whose vision has been realised.*

> [translation] Atalarımın topraklarına dönmek, modern, özgür ve demokratik Türkiye'nin kurucusuna saygılarımı sunmak ve Türkiye'nin dünyanın büyük ulusları arasında yeralmasıyla onun vizyonunun nasıl gerçekleştirildiğini görmek büyük bir ayrıcalık

Turkish rendering of an entry written in English. Milliyet and Habertürk print the same AA text. Johnson's great-grandfather Ali Kemal was an Ottoman minister who opposed the national struggle (Habertürk notes it). Foreign Secretary here, later Prime Minister. Atatürk is not named in the quoted line; 'the founder' is the reference. Evidence J. See [[Boris Johnson]].


## Ulf Kristersson (2022-11-08)

*Honoured to visit the Republic founded by Atatürk nearly a century ago; recalls photographs of Atatürk with the Swedish crown prince in 1934.*

> It is a great honour for me to visit the Republic of Turkiye, which was founded by Ataturk nearly 100 years ago

English as printed by Middle East Monitor (an Anadolu Agency photo); Odatv prints the Turkish rendering ('Yaklaşık 100 yıl önce tarafınızca kurulan Türkiye Cumhuriyeti'ni ziyaret etmek benim için büyük bir onurdur'), where the entry addresses Atatürk directly ('founded by you'). Whether the English is the original or an AA translation back from Turkish is not established. Written during the NATO accession row; the entry goes on to mention photographs of Atatürk with Crown Prince Gustaf Adolf at the 1934 opening of the Swedish Embassy. Evidence J. See [[Ulf Kristersson]].


## Lee Jae-myung (2025-11-24)

*Commemorates Atatürk and pledges to advance together for the common prosperity of the two 'blood-brother' nations.*

> [translation] Atatürk'ü anarak, kan bağıyla bağlı kardeş uluslarımızın ortak refahı için birlikte ilerleyeceğiz.

Turkish rendering (AA) of a Korean entry; the original is not located. AA, Hürriyet, Odatv, Haberler, Nefes and A Haber print the same sentence, one AA wire. Courtesy line; no evaluation of Atatürk. Evidence J. See [[Lee Jae-myung]].


## Dan Caine (2026-07-07)

*Writes for the delegation of his deep respect and admiration for Atatürk, the great and distinguished leader of the Republic.*

> [translation] Türkiye Cumhuriyeti'nin büyük ve seçkin lideri Mustafa Kemal Atatürk'e duyduğum derin saygı ve hayranlık nedeniyle, Amerikan halkının bir temsilcisi olarak Türk milletine ve bu büyük lidere saygılarımı sunmaktan büyük onur ve gurur duyuyorum.

Turkish rendering of an entry written in English. Written 'on behalf of the delegation' during the 36th NATO Heads of State and Government Summit in Ankara. Dünya, Habertürk (credited AA), Gazete Gerçek and Haberler (23 Jul 2026 repost) print the same text, one AA wire. The wording resembles Tillerson's 2017 entry ('büyük ve seçkin lideri', 'derin saygı ve hayranlık', 'bir temsilcisi'), probably a protocol template drafted by the embassy. Evidence J. See [[Dan Caine]].


## Dry ends and leads (round 16)

- **Tito, 13 April 1954:** an Instagram post (yugoslavyagocmenleri, 9 Nov 2023) and a column by Can Hacıoğlu (2eylul.com.tr, 'Mareşal Tito'nun önemi') give the line 'Sizi hayatım boyunca daima ilham kaynağı olarak yaşattım' ('I have kept you alive as an inspiration all my life'), written in a white uniform. No 1954 newspaper or book citation found, so it is not stored. Milliyet, 14 Apr 1954, p. 1 is the Wikipedia reference for the visit.
- **Koizumi, 10 January 2006:** Hürriyet (AA) prints the entry ('Türkiye Cumhuriyeti'nin kurucusu büyük Atatürk'e en derin saygılarım ve Türkiye-Japonya dostluğunun daha da gelişmesi temennisiyle'), but only one outlet was found, so it is held.
- **Stubb, 1 October 2024:** AA, Patronlar Dünyası and Haber Ekspres print the entry; Atatürk appears only as founder of the Republic in a line about the 1924 Finnish-Turkish treaty, so it was left out as a bare mention.
- **Entries not found:** Medvedev (2010), Abe (2013), Blair, Cameron (İHA, only 'signed'), Macron, Modi, Meloni, Sánchez, Orbán, Zelensky, Nazarbayev, Lukashenko (16 Apr 2019), Mirziyoyev (19 Feb 2020, signed; text not printed), Nawrocki (23 Jun 2026, signed), Rousseff (7 Oct 2011), Correa (15 Mar 2012), Prabowo, Anwar Ibrahim (Jan 2026), Duda, Vučić, Niinistö. Dan Caine's NATO-summit visit is the only one of the July 2026 delegations that printed text for a leader; the Luxembourg permanent representative also wrote that 'Yurtta sulh, cihanda sulh' reflects the alliance's spirit.
- **Pre-1990 entries (Heuss 1957, Sukarno 1959, Ayub Khan 1959, Nixon 1956, Adenauer 1954, Hussein 1960):** DuckDuckGo found no online text; the Atatürk Araştırma Merkezi Dergisi article on Indonesia (atamdergi.gov.tr 1071) has no mention of the visit. The printed volumes of *Anıtkabir Özel Defteri* (ed. Recep Cengiz, 1953–) and Milliyet/Cumhuriyet microfilm for the visit days are the way in.
- **Academic compilations:** DuckDuckGo queries for DergiPark articles on the book returned only a stylistic study of vol. 23 (Öner, Çukurova) and library catalogue records; no article that prints foreign leaders' entries was found.
- **Celebrities:** Elon Musk (9 Nov 2017), Aamir Khan (17 Oct 2017), Kevin Costner (29 Oct 2007) and Douglas Fairbanks Jr. (1954) appear in the Turkish Wikipedia list; no text of an entry was found.
