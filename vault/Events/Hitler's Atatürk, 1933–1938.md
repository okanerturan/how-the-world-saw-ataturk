# Hitler's Atatürk, 1933–1938

The [[Adolf Hitler]] node is transcript-derived; this card holds what is now **page-confirmed** in
Ihrig's study (read through search-inside snippets, Oct 2026), so the dark mirror stands on cited
ground.

- **July 1933 — "a star in the darkness."** Interviewed in July 1933, Hitler called Atatürk's Turkey
  a "star in the darkness"; the sound bite was reprinted on the front page of the *Völkischer
  Beobachter* and in other papers (Ihrig pp. 114–115). By Ihrig's account it became, with a 1941
  remark naming Turkey a role model, "the official line on Turkey" (p. 144).
- **1938 — the teacher and his students.** Ihrig prints Hitler's 1938 line that "Mussolini was his
  first and I his second student" (p. 116; restated pp. 160, 223) — Atatürk as the teacher of
  national regeneration. (A fuller wording circulates; only this clause was visible in the
  snippet re-check.) The same page reports Hitler telling the Turkish ambassador Hüsrev Gerede that
  he "was copying Atatürk."
- **The bust.** A bust of Atatürk by Josef Thorak was, in a contemporary account Ihrig quotes (his
  n. 67), "one of his cherished possessions" (p. 129); a photograph of 10 February 1937 shows Hitler
  in Thorak's workshop with the bust behind him (p. 132, fig. 3.6).
- **November 1938.** Atatürk's death dominated German front pages from 10 November, pushing even the
  directed coverage of the Putsch anniversary to pages 2–3 (p. 139). The transcript's claim that the
  coverage "eclipsed Kristallnacht" is **not** in Ihrig: no searchable hit for Kristallnacht, pogrom
  or synagogue. The juxtaposition with the pogrom of 9–10 November is the project's framing.

What he saw: not the secularist or the emancipator but the **national resurrection by a leader's
will** — the völkisch reading (RESEARCH.md §6, card 3). See [[Hamburger Tageblatt, November 1938]]
for the same reading in a Nazi paper's obituary.
