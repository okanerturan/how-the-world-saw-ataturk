# Kemal in the Chinese Peasant and Federalist Debates, 1923–1927

Two further Chinese voices on Kemal, found by phrase search of Chinese Wikisource for the transliterations 基玛尔 / 基瑪爾 / 凱末爾 and read on the page; both use Kemal as an argument in a Chinese debate rather than describing him. The same seam's other hits are already in the vault: see [[Xiangdao Reads Ankara, 1922–1924]], [[Kemal in the Kuomintang's Quarrels, 1924–1928]] and [[Kemal as a Chinese Yardstick, 1933–1940]].

## Deng Zhongxia — 1923-12-29

*Cites Kemal's Turkish revolution as proof that peasants make revolutions: Kemal 'and the others' had much peasant help, the whole country following him.*

> 土耳其的革命，基玛尔等得农民的帮助不小。
> (translation: In Turkey's revolution, Kemal and the others had no small help from the peasants.)

Wikisource page: 論農民運動. See [[Deng Zhongxia]].

## Chang Naide (常乃惪, Chang Yansheng) — 1927

*Cites General Kemal's one victory over Greece as quick national revival, then argues China's vast size makes that path a dream.*

> 如同基玛尔将军，一战胜了希腊，便可统一土耳其而复兴旧业
> (translation: as with General Kemal, who by winning one battle against Greece could unify Turkey and restore the old enterprise)

Wikisource page: 聯治救國的步驟. See [[Chang Naide]].
