# Death and State Funeral of Atatürk

The 10 Kasım project's anchor event, now with the verified timeline:

- **10 November, 9:05** — dies at Dolmabahçe Palace, Istanbul, of cirrhosis. (The morning after
  Kristallnacht; see [[Source — Reddit thread and transcript]] for the project frame.)
- **Lying in repose** — three days at Dolmabahçe, the mahogany casket flanked by six torches for
  the six arrows of [[Kemalism]]; the catafalque design is attributed to [[Bruno Taut]] in the
  architectural literature.
- **19 November** — funeral prayer, procession to Gülhane Park, torpedo boat to the battlecruiser
  **TCG Yavuz**, which carries the coffin to İzmit.
- **20 November** — funeral train to Ankara; lying in state at the Grand National Assembly.
- **21 November** — state funeral; the coffin is placed in the **Ethnography Museum of Ankara**,
  where it rests for fifteen years.
- **10 November 1953** — transferred to [[Anıtkabir]] *on the fifteenth anniversary*: the ritual
  date reproducing itself. See [[10 Kasım]].

**The delegations as data** — "28 countries" is the figure in circulation but has no contemporary
source yet; the Havas list of 17 Nov 1938 names roughly seventeen nations' special missions, plus a
League of Nations delegation. Named: [[Ioannis Metaxas]] for Greece; [[Konstantin von Neurath]] for
Germany; [[Albert Sarraut]] for France; [[Vladimir Potemkin]] for the USSR; Admiral Dudley Pound and
[[William Birdwood]] for Britain; Pompeo Aloisi for Italy; War Minister Gen. Nedić (Milutin, by tenure inference) for
Yugoslavia; "an uncle of the King" for Afghanistan. The rank each capital sent is a measurable
weight of regard — the full sourced table is [[Foreign Delegations at the Funeral, November 1938]].

*Rigour note (corrected Oct 2026): this list began as a single tertiary source (Wikipedia). Census
round 3 checked it against the Havas/Reuter wires in the Delpher corpus. Two corrections: the Yugoslav delegate was Minister Nedić — **Milutin** by inference from the tenure list (Aug 1938–Aug 1939), not his brother Milan; and
**[[Amanullah Khan]] was not among the mourners** — Afghanistan sent a royal uncle, and no source
places the exiled king in Ankara. Metaxas's attendance is announced twice in the wires (12 and
17 Nov); an eyewitness report is still wanted.*
