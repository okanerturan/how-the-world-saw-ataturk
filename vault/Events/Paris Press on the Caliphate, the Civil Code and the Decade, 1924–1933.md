# Paris Press on the Caliphate, the Civil Code and the Decade, 1924–1933

A second French card built on Gallica's page-level `ContentSearch` fragments, for the moments between the Smyrna card and the
funeral card: the caliphate (3 March 1924), the Swiss Civil Code (17 February 1926) and the republic's tenth anniversary (29 October 1933).
Fragments are short and cut with "(...)", so only what a fragment shows is claimed. The French OCR is noisy (e.g. "provo qua"); quotations are as the OCR gives them.
Context: [[Abolition of the Caliphate]], [[Turkish Civil Code]].

**La Croix** (Catholic daily). 8 March 1924, page 1 ([issue](https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/bpt6k261790c)), "Gazette": under the heading (OCR) *Le « milliard des Congrégations » en Turquie* the paper says that, "En abolissant le califat, le gouvernement d'Angora(...)"
and then: *"Ceux qui leur ont parlé du fameux milliard des Congrégations de France ont sans doute omis de leur dire comment le milliard s'est, évanoui"* — those who told Angora about the famous "billion of the Congregations" in France forgot to say how the billion vanished (and tells "l'histoire du forçat Duez"). The reading — Ankara imitating French anticlerical confiscation, and being misled by it — is an inference from a truncated paragraph.
(A 9 March item cited in the first draft of this card could not be found in the cited issue on re-check and has been withdrawn.) 6 March, page 5 ([issue](https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/bpt6k2617882)) the paper's press-review relays L'Écho de Paris (OCR-garbled) that the veneration went "non au calife, mais au sabre, et le sabre est aux mains de Ketnal" [OCR]; this is a relayed paper's view, not La Croix's own, and is not recorded.

**Le Temps**, 19 February 1926, page 6 ([issue](https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/bpt6k246148q)): the Turkish Chamber has decided unanimously to adopt the Swiss Civil Code in its entirety, and *"Ce vote consacre la séparation du statut civil et des traditions religieuses"* ("dans'la vie publique et sociale'du peuple turc" [OCR]). Kemal is not named; whether the gloss is the paper's or the dispatch's is not shown. The same day *L'Action française* p.3 sets the item under "TURQUIE La laïcisation de la Turquie", and *La Croix* p.2 prints the Swiss-code news without comment in the fragment.

**L'Action française**, 31 October 1933, page 1 ([issue](https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/bpt6k765409w)): apparently on Greek-Turkish friendship (page 2: "Ainsi naquit i'amitiê. gréco-turque" [OCR]), "Depuis l'été de 1922, où *la victoire de Mustapha Kemal provo qua la faillite catastrophique* de la mèga[lomanie]" — the fragment ends there. **Le Populaire** (Socialist), 30 October 1933, page 3 ([issue](https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/bpt6k8213624)) heads its Ankara dispatch *"L'anniversaire de la République kemaliste"*; a nearby fragment adds "De grandes fêtes ont été célébrées par les ordres de l'autorité dans toutes les villes de Turquie" (not confirmed to be the same dispatch). *L'Humanité* of 30 October, p.2, reports the military review ("Ce matin, du haut d'une tribune, Mus tapha Kemal" [OCR]) without a stated view in the fragment.

**Dry.** Franklin-Bouillon accord, 20–22 October 1921: *Le Temps* (22 Oct, p.1) says the signing is awaited and that the talks aimed "avant tout à obtenir la libération des prisonniers français"; *L'Humanité* (22 Oct, p.3) reports a rumoured French promise to help Turkey retake Smyrna and Thrace; neither fragment gives a view of Kemal, and the 23rd and 24th (Temps, AF, Humanité, Figaro, Populaire) returned no hit for "Franklin". Lausanne, 25 July 1923: *La Croix* p.5 notes that Ismet signed "avec une plume en or que lui avait envoyé d'Angora Mustapha Kemal"; *L'Humanité* p.3 says the treaty "consacre l'existence de la Turquie libre"; *L'Action française* p.1 relays Achille Mestre on the capitulations. Alphabet (Nov 1928) and women's vote (Dec 1934): nothing Kemal-related in the fragments for 2–3 Nov 1928 and 6–7 Dec 1934 (*Le Temps* 7 Dec 1934 p.3 has the Ankara measure on clerical dress and the Assembly's new seat count only). *L'Humanité* 5 Mar 1924 p.3 ("Plus de Calife, commandeur des Croyants...") has no explicit view in its fragment.
