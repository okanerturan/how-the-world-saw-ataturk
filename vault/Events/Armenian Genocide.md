# Armenian Genocide

Perpetrated by the Ottoman CUP government under [[Talat Pasha]]. The transcript's Nazi thread argues
the regime read it as a *successful precedent* — Hitler's alleged 1939 line, *"Who, after all, speaks
today of the annihilation of the Armenians?"* Also the subject of the Blue Book that [[Arnold J. Toynbee]] co-authored before switching sides, and the shadow over [[Agop Dilaçar]]'s career.
