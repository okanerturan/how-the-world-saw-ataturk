# MOC — Writers and Artists

Poets, novelists, film-makers and the people who fixed Atatürk's image in culture.

- [[Antoine de Saint-Exupéry]] — *Aviator and author* · cultural witness
- [[Can Dündar]] — *Journalist and documentarian* · the humanizer
