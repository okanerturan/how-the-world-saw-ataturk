# MOC — Antagonists and Victims

Enemies, reactionaries, purged allies, and people the revolution simply ran over.

- [[Abd al-Aziz Shawish]] — *Egyptian writer; contributor to the Cairo daily *al-Akhbar*, quoted at length in *al-Manar* 25(4), 1924* · conflicted — defends the Turks and honours Kemal's stature, blames him for abolishing the Caliphate
- [[Abdelhamid Ben Badis]] — *Algerian reformist scholar; editor of al-Shihab; founder of the Association of Algerian Muslim Ulama (1931)* · conflicted — from 'apostates' to 'the greatest man of modern history'
- [[Abdul Hamid II]] — *Sultan of the Ottoman Empire* · first jailer
- [[Abdulmejid II]] — *Last Caliph of Islam* · erased figurehead
- [[Aga Khan III]] — *Spiritual leader of the Ismaili Muslims* · petitioner, accidentally weaponised
- [[Ahmad Shawqi]] — *Poet, 'Prince of Poets'* · admirer, then devastated
- [[Ahmed al-Senussi]] — *Grand Sheikh of the Senussi Sufi order* · ally, discarded
- [[Ali Kemal]] — *Ottoman liberal journalist and Interior Minister* · domestic enemy, lynched
- [[Ali Şükrü Bey]] — *Trabzon deputy; voice of the First Assembly's opposition* · would keep Kemal in command but strip the command of the Assembly's powers; killed in Ankara, March 1923
- [[Alija Izetbegović]] — *First President of Bosnia and Herzegovina* · critic, partially converted
- [[Allama Iqbal]] — *Philosopher-poet, spiritual father of Pakistan* · conflicted admirer
- [[Antoun Saadeh]] — *Founder of the Syrian Social Nationalist Party* · ideological twin, territorial enemy
- [[Aristotle Onassis]] — *Shipping magnate* · refugee created by the victory
- [[Benito Mussolini]] — *Fascist dictator of Italy* · admirer, rebuffed; then existential threat
- [[Celadet Bedir Khan]] — *Kurdish intellectual and linguist* · mirror nationalist
- [[David Lloyd George]] — *Prime Minister of the United Kingdom* · arch-nemesis
- [[Enver Pasha]] — *Ottoman Minister of War, one of the Three Pashas* · rival, boxed out
- [[Fikriye Hanım]] — *Companion of Atatürk during the War of Independence* · personal casualty
- [[George Horton]] — *'For thirty years consul and consul-general of the United States in the Near East' (title page); in Smyrna until 13 September 1922 (Introduction)* · hostile — holds Kemal's army burned Smyrna to exterminate its Christians, and that Christian powers built him up
- [[H. E. Wortham]] — *British author of *Mustapha Kemal of Turkey* (London: Holme Press, 1930), 'Makers of the Modern Age' series* · conflicted-admiring — 'a great idealist and a great iconoclast' who 'has been entirely ruthless'
- [[H.C. Armstrong]] — *British officer; author of the first hostile biography* · hostile first biographer
- [[Haj Amin al-Husseini]] — *Grand Mufti of Jerusalem* · Nazi collaborator; bitter antagonist of Atatürk
- [[Halide Edib Adıvar]] — *Novelist, feminist, nationalist* · founding ally turned dissident
- [[Hassan al-Banna]] — *Founder and Supreme Guide of the Society of the Muslim Brothers (Ismailia, 1928)* · hostile — grieved the abolition; the Brotherhood's founding reflex
- [[Henry Morgenthau Sr.]] — *Author of *I Was Sent to Athens* (1929), on the League of Nations Greek refugee settlement (dedicated 'to the creators of the League of Nations'); former US ambassador at Constantinople (⚠️ not from the pages read)* · conflicted — 'ablest officer', 'dictator', and the man under whose eye Smyrna's Greek quarter burned
- [[Hussein bin Ali]] — *Sharif of Mecca* · ruined by Atatürk's reforms
- [[Hüseyin Avni Ulaş]] — *Erzurum deputy, First Grand National Assembly* · loyal opponent of concentrated power — hostile to the office's powers, admiring of the man's sacrifice
- [[Ihsan Nuri Pasha]] — *Kurdish commander, leader of the Republic of Ararat* · separatist antagonist
- [[Jacques Bainville]] — *French historian and Action française journalist; *Dictators* in English translation (J. Lewis May, 1937)* · ironic — a 'dictator' chapter that finds him the 'most curious and original' and mocks the speed of his reforms
- [[Kâzım Karabekir]] — *General; commander of the Eastern Army* · indispensable ally, then purged
- [[Lord Curzon]] — *British Foreign Secretary, former Viceroy of India* · defeated adversary
- [[Lothrop Stoddard]] — *American author of *The New World of Islam* (1921) and *The Rising Tide of Color** · conflicted — respects the officer and the army, fears what he reads as a rising pan-Islamic and Bolshevik-friendly resistance to the West
- [[Lysimachos Oeconomos]] — *Greek historian, Lecturer in Modern Greek and Byzantine History, King's College, University of London (title page)* · hostile — holds Kemal's troops and leaders had every interest in destroying Christian Smyrna
- [[Mahatma Gandhi]] — *Leader of the Indian independence movement* · conflicted; ultimately alienated
- [[Mehmed VI Vahideddin]] — *36th and last Sultan of the Ottoman Empire* · displaced sovereign
- [[Mehmet Akif Ersoy]] — *Poet; author of the Turkish national anthem* · ally turned voluntary exile
- [[Muammar Gaddafi]] — *Leader of Libya* · fierce detractor
- [[Mustafa Sabri Efendi]] — *Last Shaykh al-Islām of the Ottoman Empire* · theological arch-enemy
- [[Mustafa Suphi]] — *Founder and chairman of the Communist Party of Turkey* · murdered rival
- [[Nezihe Muhiddin]] — *Feminist organiser* · erased pioneer
- [[Nihal Atsız]] — *Pan-Turkist ideologue* · ultranationalist critic
- [[Nuri Dersimi]] — *Koçgiri/Dersim Kurdish nationalist, Xoybûn member, memoirist of Dersim* · hostile chronicler — the Kurdish memoirist of Dersim 1937–38
- [[Nâzım Hikmet]] — *Poet* · admirer of the general, prisoner of the state
- [[Peregrinus]] — *Pseudonymous German travel writer; *Peregrinus auf großer Fahrt, Bd. 2: Quer durch Kleinasien* (Leipzig: Otto Janke, 1932)* · conflicted — respect for the liberator, doubts about the man ('liebt nur die Türkei, seinen Staat und sich selbst')
- [[Rashid Rida]] — *Syro-Egyptian Salafi theologian; editor of al-Manar; theorist of the caliphate* · hostile — the caliphate's theorist against Ankara's secularism
- [[Rauf Orbay]] — *Ottoman naval hero; first PM of the provisional government* · founding ally, exiled dissident
- [[Rosita Forbes]] — *British traveller and author of *Conflict: Angora to Afghanistan* (1931)* · conflicted — a modernising achievement she thinks as dangerous to minorities as Abdul Hamid's fanaticism
- [[Ruhollah Khomeini]] — *Founder of the Islamic Republic of Iran* · reactionary anti-admirer
- [[Rıza Nur]] — *Lausanne delegate, minister; venomous memoirist* · hostile insider
- [[Said Nursi]] — *Islamic scholar, founder of the Nur movement* · spiritual dissident
- [[Sayyid Qutb]] — *Ideologue of the Muslim Brotherhood* · reactionary anti-admirer
- [[Seyid Riza]] — *Alevi-Kurdish tribal leader of Dersim* · rebel, executed
- [[Shakib Arslan]] — *Pan-Islamist publicist, 'Prince of Eloquence'* · hostile mirror — the pan-Islamist prosecutor
- [[Sheikh Said]] — *Zaza-Kurdish Sunni cleric and rebel leader* · armed antagonist
- [[Sisley Huddleston]] — *Author of *Those Europeans* (1924); English-born journalist writing from Paris (⚠️ biography not from the book)* · sceptical — credits Kemal's skill, doubts his power and asks whether he has led Turkey into a worse bondage
- [[Talat Pasha]] — *Ottoman Interior Minister, one of the Three Pashas* · repudiated predecessor
- [[Tunalı Hilmi]] — *Bolu deputy (1921–23) and Zonguldak deputy (1923–), First and Second Grand National Assemblies* · reliably admiring of the Commander-in-Chief in 1921; in 1924 insisted the caliphate was not 'abolished'
- [[Vecihi Hürkuş]] — *Fighter pilot and aircraft designer* · punished pioneer
- [[Zaki al-Arsuzi]] — *Arab nationalist philosopher and teacher of Antioch; League of National Action; forerunner of Ba'thism* · hostile to the Kemalist claim on the Sanjak — an oblique card with no sentence on Atatürk
- [[Ziya Hurşit]] — *Lâzistan deputy, First Grand National Assembly; later condemned in the 1926 İzmir plot* · constitutionalist critic of the Commander-in-Chief's powers; executed 14 July 1926 for the İzmir plot
- [[Şefik Hüsnü]] — *Founder of the Communist Party of Turkey (TKP)* · suppressed ally
