# MOC — Foreign Observers and Diplomats

Ambassadors, correspondents, generals and biographers — the people who explained Turkey to the
outside world, or failed to.

- [[Agatha Christie]] — *Novelist* · cultural witness
- [[Albert Sarraut]] — *French statesman; Interior Minister; head of France's funeral delegation* · admiring mourner (by his own stated word) — and, in one Dutch daily's reading, a sign of the West's courtship of Ankara
- [[Andrew Mango]] — *Biographer; BBC Turkish service head* · the standard modern biographer
- [[Antoine de Saint-Exupéry]] — *Aviator and author* · cultural witness
- [[Archibald Wavell]] — *British field marshal and biographer of Allenby* · chivalrous opponent's biographer
- [[Aristotle Onassis]] — *Shipping magnate* · refugee created by the victory
- [[Arnold J. Toynbee]] — *Historian* · propagandist turned advocate
- [[Aubrey Herbert]] — *British intelligence officer, MP, 'Lawrence of the Balkans'* · ignored prophet
- [[B.H. Liddell Hart]] — *Military historian and theorist* · professional admirer
- [[Bakr Sidqi]] — *Iraqi general; leader of the 29 Oct 1936 military coup* · admiring/instrumental — wanted Iraq's army rebuilt on the Kemalist model
- [[C.E.W. Bean]] — *Australian war correspondent at Gallipoli; official historian of the AIF* · admiring — respect for the enemy commander, made official
- [[Can Dündar]] — *Journalist and documentarian* · the humanizer
- [[Carlo Sforza]] — *Italian High Commissioner in Istanbul* · pragmatic defector
- [[Charles H. Sherrill]] — *Olympic sprinter, IOC member, US Ambassador to Turkey* · admirer / biographer
- [[Charles Harington]] — *British general, Allied commander at Constantinople 1920–1923* · professional respect with a grievance
- [[Charles Townshend]] — *British general* · defeated observer
- [[Clair Price]] — *Journalist* · explainer
- [[Clare Sheridan]] — *Sculptor and journalist* · advocate
- [[Claude Farrère and Pierre Loti]] — *French novelists and former naval officers* · advocates inside an enemy power
- [[Douglas MacArthur]] — *US Army general* · admirer
- [[Emil Ludwig]] — *Biographer* · interviewer, later admirer
- [[Enver Pasha]] — *Ottoman Minister of War, one of the Three Pashas* · rival, boxed out
- [[Ernest Hemingway]] — *Novelist; then a 23-year-old foreign correspondent* · witness
- [[Fevzi Çakmak]] — *Chief of the General Staff, Field Marshal* · loyal paradox
- [[Franklin Bouillon]] — *French diplomat and former cabinet minister* · negotiator who defected
- [[Fridtjof Nansen]] — *Polar explorer, League of Nations diplomat* · demographic engineer
- [[George Horton]] — *'For thirty years consul and consul-general of the United States in the Near East' (title page); in Smyrna until 13 September 1922 (Introduction)* · hostile — holds Kemal's army burned Smyrna to exterminate its Christians, and that Christian powers built him up
- [[Grace Ellison]] — *Writer and women's rights advocate* · advocate
- [[H. E. Wortham]] — *British author of *Mustapha Kemal of Turkey* (London: Holme Press, 1930), 'Makers of the Modern Age' series* · conflicted-admiring — 'a great idealist and a great iconoclast' who 'has been entirely ruthless'
- [[H.C. Armstrong]] — *British officer; author of the first hostile biography* · hostile first biographer
- [[Hans Kannengiesser]] — *German officer, commander of the Turkish 5th Division at Gallipoli* · respectful comrade
- [[Harold Nicolson]] — *British diplomat, biographer and diarist* · detached
- [[Henry Morgenthau Sr.]] — *Author of *I Was Sent to Athens* (1929), on the League of Nations Greek refugee settlement (dedicated 'to the creators of the League of Nations'); former US ambassador at Constantinople (⚠️ not from the pages read)* · conflicted — 'ablest officer', 'dictator', and the man under whose eye Smyrna's Greek quarter burned
- [[Herbert Melzig]] — *German author; *Kamâl Atatürk: Untergang und Aufstieg der Türkei* (Frankfurt a. M.: Societäts-Verlag, 1937)* · admiring — Atatürk as the stern schoolmaster of a nation, written under National Socialism
- [[Ishiwara Kanji]] — *Imperial Japanese Army officer, planner of the Manchurian Incident; strategist of the Tōa Renmei* · admiring in passing: Kemal as the rare ruler who held politics and strategy in one person
- [[James Harbord]] — *US Army general* · assessor
- [[John Gunther]] — *American journalist; author of the 'Inside' books* · sceptical admirer
- [[Joseph Grew]] — *First US Ambassador to the Republic of Turkey* · diarist
- [[King Edward VIII]] — *King of the United Kingdom* · reconciled monarch
- [[Kâzım Karabekir]] — *General; commander of the Eastern Army* · indispensable ally, then purged
- [[Lady Dorothy Mills]] — *British novelist and travel writer* · conflicted
- [[Lord Curzon]] — *British Foreign Secretary, former Viceroy of India* · defeated adversary
- [[Lord Kinross]] — *Historian; Patrick Balfour* · canonical Western biographer
- [[Mao Zedong]] — *Leader of the Chinese Communist Party* · student, then explicit rejecter
- [[Mark L. Bristol]] — *US High Commissioner in Istanbul* · pragmatist
- [[Mikhail Frunze]] — *Red Army commander and strategist* · ally
- [[Nicolae Titulescu]] — *Romanian diplomat, two-time President of the League of Nations* · ally
- [[Nikolaos Trikoupis]] — *Commander-in-Chief of Greek forces in Anatolia* · captured enemy, treated with chivalry
- [[Oleksandr Lototsky]] — *Ukrainian statesman, UNR envoy to Constantinople, memoirist* · admiring (memoir, 1966)
- [[Otto Liman von Sanders]] — *Prussian general commanding Ottoman forces at Gallipoli* · superior who yielded
- [[Percy Loraine]] — *British Ambassador to Turkey* · close personal friend
- [[Prince Andrew of Greece]] — *Greek general, author of Towards Disaster* · respect for an opponent
- [[Prince Takamatsu]] — *Japanese prince, brother of Emperor Hirohito* · imperial envoy
- [[Pyotr Wrangel]] — *Commander of the White Army* · stranded contemporary
- [[Rebecca West]] — *British novelist, journalist and travel writer* · aside
- [[Ruth Frances Woodsmall]] — *American YWCA leader and writer on Muslim women* · admirer (women's rights)
- [[Sabiha Gökçen]] — *World's first female combat pilot; adopted daughter of Atatürk* · engineered symbol
- [[Salvador Pardo Bolland]] — *Mexican chargé d'affaires in Turkey, 1951–1953* · sympathetic outside observer — Atatürk as the author of Turkey's Westward turn
- [[Semyon Aralov]] — *First Soviet Ambassador to Turkey* · ally, outplayed
- [[Sergei Yutkevich]] — *Film director* · hired propagandist
- [[Vecihi Hürkuş]] — *Fighter pilot and aircraft designer* · punished pioneer
- [[Vladimir Mayakovsky]] — *Futurist poet* · fellow-revolutionary observer
- [[Vladimir Potemkin]] — *Soviet First Deputy Foreign Commissar; historian of diplomacy* · instrumental — Moscow's envoy and the last guest to leave
- [[W.E.B. Du Bois]] — *Sociologist and civil rights leader* · observer / admirer
- [[William Birdwood]] — *British Field Marshal, commander of ANZAC at Gallipoli* · old enemy, mourner
