# Steve Cohen

U.S. Representative from Tennessee (Democrat), co-chair of the Congressional Turkey Caucus.

- 2012-11-13: Marking Atatürk's death anniversary, calls him the leader who made an agrarian society a modern, secular, Western-facing model: 'a great man'. See [[Congressional Record Closing Pass — King, Owen, Davis, Sirovich, Pepper, Knowland and Others]].
