# Abdullah I of Jordan

Son of [[Hussein bin Ali]], who declared the Arab Revolt against the Ottomans — so, in theory, a
lifelong enemy of the Turkish republic's founder. In fact he deeply admired Atatürk's ability to
build a cohesive modern state out of imperial collapse. He made a state visit to Ankara in 1937 and,
having been educated in Istanbul under the empire, the two men dismissed the translators and talked
for hours in fluent Ottoman Turkish. When Atatürk died the following year Abdullah wept while praying
at the Turkish embassy. Compare his brother [[Faisal I of Iraq]].
