# William H. King

U.S. Senator from Utah (Democrat).

- 1921-12-22: Presenting his Pontic Greeks resolution, says only outside force can restrain 'the bloody hands of Mustapha Kemal'. See [[Congressional Record Closing Pass — King, Owen, Davis, Sirovich, Pepper, Knowland and Others]].
- 1923-01-26: Answering Owen, calls Kemal Pasha's appeal to America 'a most adroit and intriguing document' meant to soften criticism of Kemalist treatment of Christians. See [[Congressional Record Closing Pass — King, Owen, Davis, Sirovich, Pepper, Knowland and Others]].
