# C. J. Dennis

Clarence Michael James Stanislaus Dennis wrote *The Songs of a Sentimental Bloke* and, from the 1920s, daily light verse for the Melbourne *Herald*. In 1933 he
turned the Turkish republic's tenth anniversary into a comic ode, *Kemal - The Posh Pasha*. See [[Four Poems on Atatürk — Melbourne, Damascus, Cairo and Hyderabad, 1933–1939]].
