# Sir Abdur Rahim

Allowed the condolence for a foreign ruler because Kemal's influence on Asia had 'no parallel in the history of modern times'. See [[Indian Legislative Assembly Mourns Kemal, 14 November 1938 — Desai, Nairang, Aney, Aikman, Zafar Ali Khan and Sir Abdur Rahim]].
