# Elisaveta Bagryana

Bulgaria's best-known woman poet. Her poem *Nova Atlantida* (New Atlantis) is dedicated 'To Kemal Atatürk': Ankara, 'daughter and queen of the steppes', is a risen Atlantis, the old tales of Harun al-Rashid are dead, and the legend has become life forged with hammer and sledge. Its date is not established (see the record's caveat). See [[Poets, Reformers and the Black Press on Atatürk, 1921–1935]].
