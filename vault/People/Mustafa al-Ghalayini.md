# Mustafa al-Ghalayini

The author of *Jami' al-durus al-'arabiyya*, imprisoned by the French after 1920 and a supporter of Faisal. His *Diwan* (Haifa, 1924/25) contains a poem on the Anatolian struggle in which he shows Mustafa Kemal as the leader who gathered a divided people and broke its chains, then asks: 'O Arabs! Is there not among you a Mustafa Kemal?' (quoted in Turkish translation by Ali Benli, 2021). See [[Poets, Reformers and the Black Press on Atatürk, 1921–1935]].
