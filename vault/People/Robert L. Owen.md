# Robert L. Owen

U.S. Senator from Oklahoma (Democrat).

- 1923-01-26: Introduces Kemal's address to America as 'remarkable': an educated man, speaking for Turks who, like the Founders, want freedom from outside domination. See [[Congressional Record Closing Pass — King, Owen, Davis, Sirovich, Pepper, Knowland and Others]].
