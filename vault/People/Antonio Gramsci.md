# Antonio Gramsci

In Prison Notebook 2 (§90, on the 'new evolution of Islam') Gramsci notes that Sirdar Ikbal Ali Shah explains Kemal Pasha's reforms as a return to the pure, and takes that as an example of Muslim casuistry; he sees Islam's 'national sentiment' as the coming heresy against theocratic cosmopolitanism. A search of the English Prison Notebooks vol. 1 and the Letters found no sustained judgment of Kemal. See [[Latin Pens on Mustafa Kemal — Ortega Munilla, Ortega y Gasset, Gramsci and Ledesma Ramos]].
