# Oliver Baldwin

British journalist and soldier, son of Stanley Baldwin, later Labour MP. Writing of Anatolian peasants, expects things to improve now that Kemal has limited the power of the priesthood.

> Since Mustapha Kemal has limited the power of the priesthood, things will probably be better in the future,

See [[Oliver Baldwin on Kemal, 1932]].

## Sources
- Oliver Baldwin, The Questing Beast: An Autobiography (1932), p. 129 (and p. 130 for the Turks 'may become a very different race'): https://archive.org/details/in.ernet.dli.2015.176458
