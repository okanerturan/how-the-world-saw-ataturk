# Bhulabhai Desai

Opened the Assembly's condolence on 14 November 1938: Kemal was 'one of the most courageous of world reformers', who in a day abolished the purdah. See [[Indian Legislative Assembly Mourns Kemal, 14 November 1938 — Desai, Nairang, Aney, Aikman, Zafar Ali Khan and Sir Abdur Rahim]].
