# Chang Naide

Chinese historian and journalist, Youth Party (Xingshi) ideologue. Cites General Kemal's one victory over Greece as quick national revival, then argues China's vast size makes that path a dream.

> 如同基玛尔将军，一战胜了希腊，便可统一土耳其而复兴旧业
> (translation: as with General Kemal, who by winning one battle against Greece could unify Turkey and restore the old enterprise)

See [[Kemal in the Chinese Peasant and Federalist Debates, 1923–1927]].
