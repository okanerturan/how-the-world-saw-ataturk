# Ahmad Ramzi

Egyptian writer and historian ('Bey'), seven years resident in Turkey. Writing in *al-Risala* (Cairo): Sees the Ghazi as one of the few in the East who acted after long thought; ends by predicting his 'bewildered soul' will be explained.

See [[Egyptian Pens in al-Risala on Atatürk, 1935–1946]].
