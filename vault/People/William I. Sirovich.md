# William I. Sirovich

U.S. Representative from New York (Democrat).

- 1934-04-24: Calls Kemal Pasha 'the dictator of Turkey' yet credits his reforms with creating a new nation 'dedicated to the principles of liberalism and progressivism'. See [[Congressional Record Closing Pass — King, Owen, Davis, Sirovich, Pepper, Knowland and Others]].
