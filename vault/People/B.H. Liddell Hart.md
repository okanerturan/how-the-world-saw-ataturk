# B.H. Liddell Hart

Viewed Atatürk purely through operational brilliance while British politicians lamented Turkey's
rise. Analysing his command at [[Gallipoli Campaign]] and his grand strategy in the [[Greco-Turkish War]], Hart saw the master of the "indirect approach" — a general who never wasted men in frontal
assaults when a psychological or logistical flank could be turned. He taught the campaigns in Western
military academies, arguing the rarest part of the genius was knowing exactly when to stop fighting
and start negotiating, never letting military victory outrun supply lines. Also biographer to [[T.E. Lawrence]].
