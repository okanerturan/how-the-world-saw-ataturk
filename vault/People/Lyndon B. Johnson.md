# Lyndon B. Johnson

In toasts and welcomes for İnönü (1964) and Sunay (1967) he called Atatürk the 'immortal founder of modern Turkey' and his work 'one of the great revolutions of our age'. See [[American, French and Russian Leaders on Atatürk in Their Own Words]] for the quotes.
