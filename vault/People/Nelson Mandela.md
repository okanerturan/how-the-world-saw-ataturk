# Nelson Mandela

In 1992 the Turkish government awarded him the Atatürk International Peace Prize. He refused it —
citing human rights abuses and military crackdowns against the Kurdish population in the southeast,
and saying acceptance would validate oppression. It caused a major diplomatic incident.

Seven years later, in 1999, he changed his mind, formally accepted, and sent a representative to
Ankara to collect it. The single best illustration of how geopolitically contested the Atatürk brand
remains. See [[Ihsan Nuri Pasha]], [[Celadet Bedir Khan]].
