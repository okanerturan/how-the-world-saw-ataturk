# Charles McC. Mathias

U.S. Senator from Maryland (Republican).

- 1981-07-30: Marks the centennial: Atatürk shaped Turkey 'from the ruins of a decaying empire', a charismatic rationalist and organiser whose legacy is Turkey's challenge. See [[Senate and House on Atatürk, 1957–1990 — Powell, Sikes, Mathias and Byrd]].
