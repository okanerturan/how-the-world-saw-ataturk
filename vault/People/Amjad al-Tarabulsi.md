# Amjad al-Tarabulsi

Signs 'Damascus'. His long qasida on Hagia Sophia, printed in *al-Risala* (Cairo) in August 1935, turns on Atatürk himself. See [[Four Poems on Atatürk — Melbourne, Damascus, Cairo and Hyderabad, 1933–1939]], [[Abolition of the Caliphate]].
