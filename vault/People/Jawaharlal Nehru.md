# Jawaharlal Nehru

Followed the [[Turkish War of Independence]] closely from India and, while imprisoned by the British,
wrote to his daughter Indira about world history — devoting glowing passages to "Kemal Pasha" as a
beacon of hope for all Asia. Atatürk's victory over the British and Greeks, he wrote, injected a
massive dose of confidence into the Indian independence movement. Compare [[Subhas Chandra Bose]],
who drew the militarist lesson, and [[Mahatma Gandhi]], who recoiled from it.
