# S. Radhakrishnan

Named 'Kemal Pasha's defiant creation of a secular Turkey' in January 1947 as proof that religious states were finished. See [[Constituent Assembly and Lok Sabha on Atatürk, 1946–1966 — Radhakrishnan, Gour, Kamath, Anthony and Brajeshwar Prasad]].
