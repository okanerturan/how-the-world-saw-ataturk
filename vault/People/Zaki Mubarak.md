# Zaki Mubarak

Egyptian writer and critic (then teaching in Baghdad). Writing in *al-Risala* (Cairo): Asks why no delegation goes to the Ghazi to remind him that grudge against the Arabs should not erase their gift of faith to Turkey. Recalls that 'the late Mustafa Kemal' claimed Adam and Eve were Turks; he found the claim monstrous and wrote to Kemal that they were Arabs.

See [[Egyptian Pens in al-Risala on Atatürk, 1935–1946]].
