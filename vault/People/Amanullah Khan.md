# Amanullah Khan

Visited Ankara in 1928, was mesmerised, and returned to Kabul determined to modernise Afghanistan
overnight: mandatory Western clothing, women's education, an assault on tribal and religious
authority. He lacked what Atatürk had — a battle-tested loyal army and the prestige of a war hero.
The reforms triggered a massive conservative revolt and he abdicated and fled in 1929. (A coda the record corrects: an
uncited line in circulation puts the exiled king among the mourners at Atatürk's 1938 funeral,
but the contemporary Havas wire names Afghanistan's delegate as "an uncle of the King", Zahir
Shah's family — see [[Foreign Delegations at the Funeral, November 1938]].) His wife
[[Soraya Tarzi]] and his father-in-law [[Mahmud Tarzi]] fell with him.
