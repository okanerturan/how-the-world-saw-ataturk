# Douglas MacArthur

Met Atatürk on 27 September 1932 — at Dolmabahçe Palace in Istanbul, not Ankara as the transcript
has it — in a meeting arranged through Ambassador [[Charles H. Sherrill]]. The famous "prophecy"
attached to it (Versailles solved nothing; a German war by 1940–41; the Soviet Union as the true
victor, swallowing Eastern Europe) has no contemporary source: its earliest known appearance is
*Cumhuriyet*, 8 November 1951, and Turkish researchers judge the text a Cold War-era fabrication
that contradicts the Interior Ministry's contemporaneous telegram summarising the meeting. The
signed-photograph-on-his-desk detail rests on the same later tellings.

The meeting is real; the prophecy is reception history — an Atatürk manufactured for the Cold War,
which makes it data for this vault rather than waste.
