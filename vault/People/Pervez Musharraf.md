# Pervez Musharraf

Spent seven childhood years in Ankara while his father was a diplomat, spoke fluent Turkish, and
called Atatürk his greatest hero. After seizing power in the 1999 coup he pushed "Enlightened
Moderation" — a direct echo of [[Kemalism]] aimed at curbing Islamic extremism. Lacking Atatürk's
foundational legitimacy and facing an entrenched religious establishment, the secularising ambition
largely failed. The 21st-century entry in the long list beginning with [[Reza Shah Pahlavi]].
