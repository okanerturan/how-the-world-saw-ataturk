# John Gunther

Chicago Daily News correspondent in Europe and author of *Inside Europe* (1936), a bestseller that profiled the continent's dictators one chapter at a time. His chapter on Atatürk, 'The Turkish Colossus', calls him 'the roughneck of dictators' and 'the dictator-type carried to its ultimate extreme', and also the man who 'took a nation that was centuries deep in rot, pulled it to its feet, wiped its face, re-clothed it, transformed it, made it work'. See [[Famous Pens on Atatürk — Gunther, Dewey, Nicolson, Coudenhove-Kalergi, West]].
