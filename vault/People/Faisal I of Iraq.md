# Faisal I of Iraq

Principal military commander of the Arab Revolt, fighting alongside [[T.E. Lawrence]] in a brutal
guerrilla war to destroy the Ottoman army in the Middle East. By 1931, as King of Iraq under British
mandate, he had concluded the real threat to Arab sovereignty was not the Turks but British and
French imperialism. In an extraordinary pivot he made a publicised state visit to Ankara to meet his
former mortal enemy; the two found deep common ground and forged a friendship pact that permanently
stabilised the Turkish-Iraqi border. Brother of [[Abdullah I of Jordan]].
