# Ali al-Tantawi

Syrian writer and jurist (writing from Mecca). Writing in *al-Risala* (Cairo): On Hagia Sophia: the page of Islam was closed there 'in the name of this ... Atatürk', as it opened in Muhammad the Conqueror's name.

See [[Egyptian Pens in al-Risala on Atatürk, 1935–1946]].
