# Tom Lantos

U.S. Representative from California (Democrat), ranking member of the House International Relations Committee.

- 2005-09-27: Says he urged Musharraf to follow 'the great modernizer' Atatürk, and that Atatürk would admire Musharraf's path. See [[Congressional Record Pens on Atatürk — Connally, Pepper, Levin, Moynihan, Byrd, Lantos and Others]].
- 2006-05-19: Honours Atatürk on his 125th birth anniversary as the revolutionary who showed Islam and modernity are compatible, an example 'for our own era'. See [[Congressional Record Pens on Atatürk — Connally, Pepper, Levin, Moynihan, Byrd, Lantos and Others]].
