# Felix Faris

Lebanese-Egyptian essayist and polemicist. Writing in *al-Risala* (Cairo): Calls Atatürk 'the shrewd' who knew the Turkish people's nature, a reformer who weakened every element out of tune with Turkey's spirit.

See [[Egyptian Pens in al-Risala on Atatürk, 1935–1946]].
