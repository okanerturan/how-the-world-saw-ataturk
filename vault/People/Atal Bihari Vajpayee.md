# Atal Bihari Vajpayee

Prime Minister of India. Wrote in the Anıtkabir special book on 2003-09-17: "I come to pay homage to a great patriot, an intrepid warrior and a charismatic leader. Mustafa Kemal Ataturk led his people through the darkest hours of war and invasion, to freedom and into the modern age." See [[Anıtkabir Special Book, 2003–2007 — Vajpayee, Olmert, Katsav, Verhofstadt, Balkenende and Others]].
