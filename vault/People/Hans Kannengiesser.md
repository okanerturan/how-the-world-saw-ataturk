# Hans Kannengiesser

He took over the 5th Division at Gallipoli in August 1915 and remembered Kemal there as 'a clear-thinking, active, quiet man who knew what he wanted'. See [[Soldiers on Mustafa Kemal in Their Own Words, 1915–1940]] for the quote and sources.
