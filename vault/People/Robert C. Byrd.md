# Robert C. Byrd

U.S. Senator from West Virginia (Democrat), President pro tempore emeritus.

- 2007-10-29: On Republic Day, describes Mustafa Kemal, later Atatürk, as a war hero who rejected the outdated modes of empire and chose reform and modernisation. See [[Congressional Record Pens on Atatürk — Connally, Pepper, Levin, Moynihan, Byrd, Lantos and Others]].
