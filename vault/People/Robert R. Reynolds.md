# Robert R. Reynolds

U.S. Senator from North Carolina.

- 1939-04-13: In an aside on Mussolini's seizure of Albania, calls Atatürk 'that great man' who did more for Turkey than any other since its founding. See [[Congressional Record Pens on Atatürk — Connally, Pepper, Levin, Moynihan, Byrd, Lantos and Others]].
