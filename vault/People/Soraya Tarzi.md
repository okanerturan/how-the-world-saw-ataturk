# Soraya Tarzi

Accompanied [[Amanullah Khan]] on the 1928 Turkey visit and was profoundly moved by Turkish women
entering universities and professions. Back in Kabul she tore off her veil in public at a Loya Jirga
— with her husband's backing — and pushed to abolish polygamy and open girls' schools. That rapid
Kemalist-inspired push enraged the conservative tribal leadership and helped ignite the civil war
that exiled them both to Europe.
