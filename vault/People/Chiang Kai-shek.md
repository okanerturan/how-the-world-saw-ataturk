# Chiang Kai-shek

China and the Ottoman Empire were both "Sick Men" — vast ancient empires being carved up. The KMT
obsessively studied Atatürk's success: both movements were trying to unify fractured countries, expel
foreign imperialists and force rapid modernisation. Under Chiang, the party translated Atatürk's
speeches and biographies into Chinese and made them required reading for military officers and party
officials, treating Turkey as proof that an "inferior" non-Western nation could modernise fast and
rewrite unequal treaties by will and discipline. Compare his enemy [[Mao Zedong]], who studied the
same case and rejected it.
