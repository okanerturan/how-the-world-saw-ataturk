# Édouard Herriot

French Radical leader, former Prime Minister and mayor of Lyon. After a 1933 visit, calls Kemal a remarkable statesman who believes in science and reason, a disciple perhaps unconscious of Comte and Descartes.

> Le Ghazi croit à la science, à la raison. Je vous l'ai dit : je me le représente comme un disciple, inconscient peut-être, d'Auguste Comte et de Descartes.
> (translation: The Ghazi believes in science, in reason. I told you: I picture him as a disciple, unconscious perhaps, of Auguste Comte and of Descartes.)

See [[French Pens on Kemal, 1920–1944 — Herriot, Pernot, Pinon, Delarue-Mardrus and Franklin-Bouillon]].
