# Orhan Pamuk

Istanbul novelist whose *Snow* (2002) fills a provincial town with Atatürk monuments and 'Atatürk and painting' lectures, and whose memoir *Istanbul* (2003) remembers the 'staunch disciples of Atatürk' who ran the press. In his essay on André Gide's Turkish travels in *Other Colors* (2007) he reads the Hat Law as a way of 'striking at the heart of shame', Atatürk finding 'a way of confronting the shame suffered by all' Turks. See [[Famous Pens on Atatürk II — Farrère, Halide Edib, Toynbee, Pamuk, Nesin]] and [[Hat Law]].
