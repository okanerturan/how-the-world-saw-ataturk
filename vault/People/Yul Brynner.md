# Yul Brynner

Cast to play Atatürk in a planned film, he visited Anıtkabir in April 1962, wrote 'Size karşı büyük bir saygı ve hürmet duyuyorum' in the special book and told reporters: 'Atatürk is a true hero. Putting myself in his place makes me shiver.' The film was never made. See [[Anıtkabir Special Book, 2003–2007 — Vajpayee, Olmert, Katsav, Verhofstadt, Balkenende and Others]].
