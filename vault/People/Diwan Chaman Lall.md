# Diwan Chaman Lall

In March 1924 credited 'the Sword of Mustapha Kemal Pasha', not the Government of India, with the revision of the Treaty of Sevres. See [[Indian Legislative Assembly on Kemal, 1924–1925 — Chaman Lall and Motilal Nehru]].
