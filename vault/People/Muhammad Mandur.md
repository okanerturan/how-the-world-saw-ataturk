# Muhammad Mandur

Egyptian literary critic. Writing in *al-Risala* (Cairo): Cites Mustafa Kemal and Stalin as proof that determined will overcomes obstacles regardless of collective mind or social laws.

See [[Egyptian Pens in al-Risala on Atatürk, 1935–1946]].
