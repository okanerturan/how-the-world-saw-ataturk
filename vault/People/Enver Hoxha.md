# Enver Hoxha

Between 1967 and 1982 he named Atatürk four times, each time favourably: as the victor over Venizelos's forces, a 'great statesman', and an 'outstanding democrat' who destroyed the Caliphate. See [[Hoxha, Beneš, Brezhnev and the Soviet Presidium on Atatürk, 1939–1982]] for the quotes and sources.
