# Adam Clayton Powell Jr.

U.S. Representative from New York (Harlem).

- 1957-08-27: Sends Republic Day greetings and sketches Atatürk's revolution, calling the transformation of Turkey since 1919 admirable and its modernisation 'the goal of Kemal Ataturk'. See [[Senate and House on Atatürk, 1957–1990 — Powell, Sikes, Mathias and Byrd]].
