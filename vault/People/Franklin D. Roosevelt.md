# Franklin D. Roosevelt

Frequently expressed a wish to meet Atatürk, telling the Turkish ambassador he regarded him as one of
the most remarkable and valuable leaders of the 20th century — a stabilising force in a chaotic
world. See also [[Eleanor Roosevelt]], whose interest was in the suffrage record rather than the
geopolitics.
