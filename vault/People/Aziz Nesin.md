# Aziz Nesin

Turkey's best-known satirist, jailed under several governments. His private letters to the critic Tahsin Saraç, published as *Aziz Nesin - Tahsin Saraç Mektuplaşmaları* (1995), include, in May 1987, a list of what he is glad Turkey has: Nasreddin Hoca, Yunus, Sinan, Nâzım, and Mustafa Kemal. 'Ben en çok Mustafa Kemal'e şaşarım' ('Mustafa Kemal astonishes me most'), he writes, a genius who rose out of that swamp 'despite all his human shortcomings'. See [[Famous Pens on Atatürk II — Farrère, Halide Edib, Toynbee, Pamuk, Nesin]] and [[Nâzım Hikmet]].
