# Abbas Mahmud al-Aqqad

Egyptian essayist, poet and critic. Writing in *al-Risala* (Cairo): Holds Mustafa Kemal up against Hitler: he spent a fraction of Hitler's armaments yet retook Istanbul with the Allied armies in it.

See [[Egyptian Pens in al-Risala on Atatürk, 1935–1946]].
