# Jean-Thomas Nordmann

Member of the European Parliament (ELDR) 1999-2004.

## Jean-Thomas Nordmann — 2004-04-01

Defending Turkish secularism against the rapporteur, calls it perhaps the most valuable part of Kemal Atatürk's legacy and a recipe for the Union's future.

> Paragraph 32 of your report seems to me to proceed from a slightly jaundiced view of the secularism which is perhaps the most valuable part of the legacy of Kemal Atatürk.

**Source.** European Parliament, Strasbourg, plenary 1 April 2004, 'Progress towards accession by Turkey' (Oostlander report), verbatim report CRE-5-2004-04-01 item 2 — <https://www.europarl.europa.eu/doceo/document/CRE-5-2004-04-01-ITM-002_EN.html>

**Caveat.** One sentence of a short intervention on the Oostlander report. The report prints only 'Nordmann (ELDR)'; the first name is the standard record (M-tier).
