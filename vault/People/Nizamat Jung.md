# Nizamat Jung

Contributor to *Islamic Culture* (Hyderabad), whose first number of the new Hyderabad series (January 1939) closes its articles with his English poem on Mustafa Kemal. See [[Four Poems on Atatürk — Melbourne, Damascus, Cairo and Hyderabad, 1933–1939]], [[Death and State Funeral of Atatürk]].
