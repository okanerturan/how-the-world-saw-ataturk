# Sayyid Qutb

Built his radical ideology as a direct reactionary response to [[Kemalism]]. The 1924 [[Abolition of the Caliphate]] and the stripping of Islamic law from the Turkish constitution were, to him, the most
catastrophic and humiliating events in modern Islamic history. He wrote extensively on the "poison"
of Western secular nationalism injected into the Muslim world. His entire political project — a state
governed strictly by religious law — was designed to reverse the Kemalist template. See [[Ruhollah Khomeini]], [[Mustafa Sabri Efendi]], [[Said Nursi]].
