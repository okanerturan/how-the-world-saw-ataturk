# Jeremy Corbyn

In March 1988 he blamed 'the rise of Kemal Ataturk' for the non-implementation of the Sèvres provisions on the Kurds; he repeated the point in June 1991. See [[Westminster on Atatürk, 1988–2004 — Corbyn, MacShane and Patten]].
