# Richard Coudenhove-Kalergi

Austrian count who founded the Pan-European Union in 1923. In his 1943 autobiography *Crusade for Pan-Europe* he names Atatürk a 'great leader' whose Turkey took up Western civilization 'without any European pressure', and elsewhere warns that Kemal's defiance of Sèvres taught Germany, Hungary and Bulgaria to demand treaty revision 'a la Kemal'. He also reports that [[Eleftherios Venizelos]] told him in Vienna that Kemal's Turkey had become 'an integral part of Western civilization'. See [[Famous Pens on Atatürk — Gunther, Dewey, Nicolson, Coudenhove-Kalergi, West]].
