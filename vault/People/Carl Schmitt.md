# Carl Schmitt

Where [[Adolf Hitler]] admired the battlefield and the ethnic policy, Schmitt admired the
constitutional ruthlessness. Obsessed with the *state of exception* — the sovereign is he who can
suspend the law to save the state — he distinguished in *Die Diktatur* between the *commissarial*
dictator, who takes emergency powers to protect an existing constitution, and the *sovereign*
dictator, who uses absolute power to destroy an old legal order and forge a new one.

Atatürk was his exemplary modern sovereign dictator: he did not suspend Ottoman law, he annihilated
it and birthed an entirely new secular constitution in its place ([[Turkish Civil Code]]). Schmitt
used that success to philosophically justify the Nazi dismantling of the Weimar Republic.
