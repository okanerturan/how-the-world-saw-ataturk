# Refik Halid Karay

An Istanbul official of 1919 who was exiled by Ankara and wrote in his memoir that Kemal's rivals in the capital were people of small capacity. See [[Rivals, Exiles and Islamist Critics on Mustafa Kemal in Their Own Words, 1920–1967]] for the quotes and sources.
