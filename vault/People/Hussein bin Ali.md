# Hussein bin Ali

Launched the Arab Revolt with [[T.E. Lawrence]], dreaming of ruling a united Arab kingdom as Caliph.
The British betrayed him and carved up the Middle East instead.

Then in March 1924 came the [[Abolition of the Caliphate]], and Hussein immediately declared himself
the new Caliph of all Muslims. It was a catastrophic miscalculation: the Islamic world, already
sceptical of a man who had allied with the Christian British against the Ottomans, rejected him
outright, and his rival Ibn Saud used the unpopular claim as pretext for war. Within months the
Saudis had taken Mecca and driven him into exile. A pen stroke in Ankara inadvertently finished the
Hashemite dream in Arabia. Father of [[Abdullah I of Jordan]] and [[Faisal I of Iraq]].
