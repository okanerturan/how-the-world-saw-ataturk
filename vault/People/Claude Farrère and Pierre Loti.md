# Claude Farrère and Pierre Loti

Two enormously popular French writers who became ardent public defenders of Atatürk in the French
press *while France was occupying Turkish territory*. Farrère travelled to Turkey at the height of the
War of Independence in 1922 to meet him personally. This infuriated the British and helped legitimise
the Turkish resistance with the European public — accelerating France's decision to sign a separate
peace and withdraw. Atatürk essentially weaponised their celebrity to split Western opinion. Compare
[[Franklin Bouillon]], the diplomat who did the same from inside the government.
