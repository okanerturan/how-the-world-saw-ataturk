U.S. Senator from New York (Democrat).

- 1999-10-18: Opens a Turkey free-trade bill with Atatürk as 'legendary': he built a western-oriented, secular state from the ruins of the Ottoman Empire. See [[Congressional Record Pens on Atatürk — Connally, Pepper, Levin, Moynihan, Byrd, Lantos and Others]].
