# David Ben-Gurion

Lived in Istanbul before WWI studying law, and was deeply marked by the fierce nationalism of the
Young Turks and then Atatürk. He read Kemalist Turkey as a template for Israel: a state that
completely reinvents its own people. As Atatürk forged a secular "New Turk," severed from the Islamic
Ottoman past and speaking a purified Turkish, Ben-Gurion wanted a secular, martial "New Jew," severed
from European exile and speaking modernised Hebrew. Compare his political opposite [[Ze'ev Jabotinsky]], who drew the same lesson from the militarist end.
