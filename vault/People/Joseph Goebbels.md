# Joseph Goebbels

His diary notes, on İnönü's election in November 1938, that the new president is well disposed to Germany, 'which could not always be said of Kemal Pasha'. See [[Statesmen on Atatürk in Their Own Words, Memoirs and Speeches]] for the quote and sources.
