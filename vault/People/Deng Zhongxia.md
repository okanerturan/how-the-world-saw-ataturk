# Deng Zhongxia

Early Chinese Communist and labour-movement leader (writing in Zhongguo Qingnian). Cites Kemal's Turkish revolution as proof that peasants make revolutions: Kemal 'and the others' had much peasant help, the whole country following him.

> 土耳其的革命，基玛尔等得农民的帮助不小。
> (translation: In Turkey's revolution, Kemal and the others had no small help from the peasants.)

See [[Kemal in the Chinese Peasant and Federalist Debates, 1923–1927]].
