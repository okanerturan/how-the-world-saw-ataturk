# Ze'ev Jabotinsky

The right-wing mirror of [[David Ben-Gurion]]: founder of the militant ideology that eventually
produced Likud, fascinated by Atatürk's uncompromising militarism and total unsentimental break with
the past. Believing European Jews had been made weak by exile, he wanted them to undergo a Kemalist
transformation — a militarised society elevating nation-state and self-defence above religious
tradition, with Atatürk's purging of Islamic law as the exact model for casting off rabbinical
constraint.
