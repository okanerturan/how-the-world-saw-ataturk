# Jan Peter Balkenende

Prime Minister of the Netherlands. Wrote in the Anıtkabir special book on 2003-10-08: "I am delighted and honoured to be here, at this historic moment in Turkey's European aspirations, as articulated for the Young Republic by its Founder, Kemal Atatürk." See [[Anıtkabir Special Book, 2003–2007 — Vajpayee, Olmert, Katsav, Verhofstadt, Balkenende and Others]].
