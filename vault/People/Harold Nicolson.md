# Harold Nicolson

Diplomat and writer whose *Curzon: The Last Phase* (1934) is the standard British account of the Lausanne years seen from the Foreign Office. On Mustafa Kemal he is unsentimental: the career is 'typically Young Turk', and the luck came from the enmities. See [[Famous Pens on Atatürk — Gunther, Dewey, Nicolson, Coudenhove-Kalergi, West]] and [[Lord Curzon]].
