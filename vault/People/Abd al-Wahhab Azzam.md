# Abd al-Wahhab Azzam

Egyptian orientalist and professor (later Egypt's minister to Saudi Arabia). Writing in *al-Risala* (Cairo): In a serial against 'the Kemalists', says Muslims counted the Ghazi their undisputed hero, yet he led the Kemalists' break with Islam.

See [[Egyptian Pens in al-Risala on Atatürk, 1935–1946]].
