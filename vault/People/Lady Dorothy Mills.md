# Lady Dorothy Mills

She visited Angora in 1925 without meeting the Ghazi and wrote of him as 'a God to his followers' with a dictator's power. See [[Western Women Commentators on Atatürk, 1922–1936 — Sheridan, Mills, Woodsmall]] for the quotes and sources.
