# Edvard Beneš

In his Chicago lectures (1939) he lists Atatürk's dictatorship among the interwar dictatorships, but credits his victory and 'courageous modernisation' for its authority. See [[Hoxha, Beneš, Brezhnev and the Soviet Presidium on Atatürk, 1939–1982]] for the quotes and sources.
