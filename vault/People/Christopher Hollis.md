# Christopher Hollis

In the Trieste debate of 28 October 1953 he credited 'the statesman Kemal Ataturk' with ending the Greek-Turkish enmity. See [[Westminster on Atatürk, 1941–1957 — Eden, Selwyn Lloyd, Noel-Baker, Wigg, Davies and Hollis]].
