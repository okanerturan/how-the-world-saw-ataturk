# Virginia Foxx

U.S. Representative from North Carolina (Republican), co-chair of the Turkish Study Group.

- 2009-05-19: On his 128th birthday calls Atatürk 'a unique and inspirational figure', an impatient reformer, and urges Muslim leaders to take inspiration from his revolution. See [[Congressional Record Closing Pass — King, Owen, Davis, Sirovich, Pepper, Knowland and Others]].
