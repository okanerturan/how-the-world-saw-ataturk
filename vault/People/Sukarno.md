# Sukarno

Facing the task of uniting a Muslim-majority archipelago containing large Hindu, Christian and
Buddhist populations, he studied the [[Abolition of the Caliphate]] extensively and used Turkey as his
argument against Indonesian clerics demanding an Islamic state: a Muslim-majority nation, he
insisted, could only reach modern greatness and internal peace under a strictly secular constitution.
That reasoning underlies Pancasila.
