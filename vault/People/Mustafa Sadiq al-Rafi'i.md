# Mustafa Sadiq al-Rafi'i

Egyptian essayist and traditionalist man of letters (Tanta). Writing in *al-Risala* (Cairo): Through a fictional woman interlocutor: Mustafa Kemal is a rebel, not a reformer, a commander made vain by victory, whose hatred of religion proves it.

See [[Egyptian Pens in al-Risala on Atatürk, 1935–1946]].
