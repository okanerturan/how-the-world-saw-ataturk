# Margaret Thatcher

British Prime Minister who, in speeches and an interview around her April 1988 visit to Ankara (the first full official visit by a British premier), named Atatürk among the towering figures of history. See [[Western Statesmen and Churchmen on Atatürk in Their Own Words]] for the quotes.
