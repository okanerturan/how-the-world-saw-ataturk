# Winston Churchill

As First Lord of the Admiralty he orchestrated the 1915 [[Gallipoli Campaign]] that Atatürk thwarted,
handing Britain a humiliating defeat. He held immense respect for his former battlefield rival
regardless, publicly mourning him on his death as a "great hero" and "warrior prince" who saved his
country from ruin — a loss he called devastating for Europe.

Was also infuriated by his own cousin, [[Clare Sheridan]], for going to Ankara and coming back a
Kemalist.
