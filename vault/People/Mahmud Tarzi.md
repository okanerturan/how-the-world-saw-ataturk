# Mahmud Tarzi

The intellectual engine behind the Afghan modernisation project. Shaped in Ottoman exile by the Young
Turks, he returned as Foreign Minister and chief ideologue, pushing women's rights and European-style
reform — trying, essentially, to be the Atatürk of Central Asia. When the 1929 conservative rebellion
annihilated his life's work he fled to the one place that understood the vision. Atatürk granted him
asylum, and he died in Istanbul watching from the sidelines as someone else successfully built the
republic he had failed to secure. Father-in-law of [[Amanullah Khan]] via [[Soraya Tarzi]].
