# Falih Rıfkı Atay

The most influential journalist of the republican era, at Atatürk's side from 1923 to 1938 — at the
dinner tables, on the train journeys, inside the reform debates. His memoir *Çankaya* (book form
1961) narrates the whole arc from birth to death with the texture only proximity buys, and it is
the quarry from which most later biographies — [[Lord Kinross]] and [[Andrew Mango]] included —
mined their humanizing anecdotes.

His place in the deck: the **loyal insider's card**. *Çankaya* is devoted. ⚠️ The common claim that it is "not embalmed" — that it also
preserves the drinking, the tempers and the loneliness alongside the genius — is not shown by the pages
cited here (a biography entry and edition listings) and is unchecked against the text. Read against [[Rıza Nur]] (the
poisoned version of the same proximity) and [[Nutuk]] (the self-authorized version).
