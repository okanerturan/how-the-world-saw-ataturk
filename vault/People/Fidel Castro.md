# Fidel Castro

A vocal and passionate admirer despite Atatürk's suppression of communists, seeing him as a pioneer of
anti-imperialist guerrilla warfare and state-building. The bust is verified: it stands in Havana at
Castro's behest, by the Turkish sculptor Metin Yurdanur, inscribed *"Yurtta barış, dünyada barış."*
His 1997 remarks to Turkish visitors circulate in several incompatible wordings — "a star that fell
on your country," "the only leader who governed his country even after his death" — and the popular
*"I am a follower of Atatürk"* phrasing has no primary source. The reported origin of the interest:
[[Nâzım Hikmet]] visited Havana in 1961, told Castro about the War of Independence, and Castro
requested a copy of *Nutuk* from the Turkish embassy.
