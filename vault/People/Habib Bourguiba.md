# Habib Bourguiba

Led Tunisia out of French rule and modelled his presidency explicitly on Atatürk: aggressive
secularisation, abolition of polygamy, expanded women's rights, and a one-party system built straight
off the Kemalist blueprint. One of the more durable copies. See [[Kemalism]].
