# Manfred Weber

CSU member of the European Parliament, EPP Group leader.

## Manfred Weber — 2016-11-22

After the coup attempt, recalls 'a great Turk', Atatürk, citing his sayings on peace and fear-free sovereignty as Turkey's own tradition.

> Ich möchte heute in meinem Redebeitrag an einen großen Türken erinneren, nämlich an Atatürk. Peace at home – peace in the world, war einer seiner Sätze, die er geprägt hat.
>
> [Translation:] In my speech today I would like to recall a great Turk, namely Atatürk. 'Peace at home - peace in the world' was one of the sayings he coined.

**Source.** European Parliament, Strasbourg, plenary 22 November 2016, 'EU-Turkey relations (debate)', verbatim report CRE-8-2016-11-22 item 10 — <https://www.europarl.europa.eu/doceo/document/CRE-8-2016-11-22-ITM-010_EN.html>

**Caveat.** Original German as in the verbatim report ('erinneren' is the report's misspelling). He goes on to quote Atatürk's line that sovereignty built on fear and arms alone cannot last, and to tell Ankara to argue from Turkey's own tradition rather than 'our European values'; the praise serves his message to Erdoğan's government, hence instrumental. Role: the report names him 'im Namen der PPE-Fraktion'; his CSU membership is the standard record.
