# Bob Hawke

Prime Minister of Australia (1983–1991). Speeches on Atatürk and Gallipoli; see [[Anzac Day Leaders on Atatürk, 1990–2024 — Australian and New Zealand Speeches]] and [[Johnnies and Mehmets]].

- 1990-04-25 — Speech at Lone Pine Ceremony, Gallipoli, 25 April 1990: At Lone Pine on the 75th anniversary, honours the Turkish dead as inspired by the leadership of a 'Man of Destiny', Kemal Atatürk. <https://pmtranscripts.pmc.gov.au/release/transcript-8010>
- 1991-05-09 — Speech at Parliamentary dinner for President Özal of Turkey, Canberra, 9 May 1991: Tells President Özal that Atatürk's leadership and skill at Gallipoli paved the way for his building of the modern Turkish state. <https://pmtranscripts.pmc.gov.au/release/transcript-8295>
