# William F. Knowland

U.S. Senator from California, Majority Leader (Republican).

- 1953-10-30: Judging Egypt's Naguib after an Asian tour, says he has 'some of the zeal that Kemal Ataturk had' in modernising Turkey. See [[Congressional Record Closing Pass — King, Owen, Davis, Sirovich, Pepper, Knowland and Others]].
