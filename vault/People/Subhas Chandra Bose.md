# Subhas Chandra Bose

Sought German support for an Indian army against the British. Met Hitler in 1942 and got nothing of
substance — not really a racial matter: *Mein Kampf* is explicit that the British Empire was a
civilisational achievement worth preserving precisely because it kept Indians under European control.
Anti-colonial nationalism interested Hitler only as an occasional weapon against Britain.

**Long before Berlin, there was Ankara.** Against [[Mahatma Gandhi]]'s non-violence, Bose held that
empires respect only force, and cited "Kemal Pasha" as the exact type India needed — a ruthless,
uncompromising modernizer who would build a disciplined army, eject the British violently and drag a
fractured society into the modern era. His admiration for Atatürk's militarised nationalism was a
major source of friction with the pacifist wing of the independence movement.
