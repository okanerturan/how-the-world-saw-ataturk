# Tony Abbott

Prime Minister of Australia (2013–2015). Speeches on Atatürk and Gallipoli; see [[Anzac Day Leaders on Atatürk, 1990–2024 — Australian and New Zealand Speeches]] and [[Johnnies and Mehmets]].

- 2015-04-22 — Joint press conference with Prime Minister Davutoğlu, Ankara, 22 April 2015: Calls Atatürk's message to the grieving mothers of Australia wise and magnanimous, from a 'truly great inspirational leader' whose legacy lives on. <https://pmtranscripts.pmc.gov.au/release/transcript-24389>
- 2015-04-24 — Doorstop interview, HMAS Anzac, Anzac Cove, 24 April 2015: Credits the Atatürk legacy for making peaceful, prosperous, reasonably pluralist Turkey a beacon to the Islamic world. <https://pmtranscripts.pmc.gov.au/release/transcript-24393>
