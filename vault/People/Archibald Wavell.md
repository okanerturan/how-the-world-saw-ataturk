# Archibald Wavell

In his Allenby biography (1940) he names Kemal among the two best Turkish commanders of 1918 and the leader 'who was to regenerate the Turkish nation'. See [[Soldiers on Mustafa Kemal in Their Own Words, 1915–1940]] for the quote and sources.
