# George Wigg

A soldier in Constantinople in 1923, he returns to Kemal across 1946–57; on 5 February 1957 he reminds the Commons that Britain tried to knock Kemal Ataturk down in 1922 'and they failed'. See [[Westminster on Atatürk, 1941–1957 — Eden, Selwyn Lloyd, Noel-Baker, Wigg, Davies and Hollis]].
