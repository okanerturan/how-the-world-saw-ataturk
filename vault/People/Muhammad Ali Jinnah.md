# Muhammad Ali Jinnah

The admiration was slightly paradoxical: the [[Abolition of the Caliphate]] outraged much of Muslim
India, but Jinnah — a secularly minded lawyer fighting for an independent Muslim state — revered him
as the ultimate anti-imperialist who restored dignity to the East. On Atatürk's death he organised
widespread mourning across India, calling him *"the greatest Musalman in the modern Islamic world."*
Compare his ideological counterpart [[Allama Iqbal]], whose admiration curdled.
