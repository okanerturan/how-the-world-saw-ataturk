# Rebecca West

In *Black Lamb and Grey Falcon* (1941), a book about Yugoslavia, Atatürk appears by the way, in several asides. She sets the Turks of Ottoman Macedonia against 'the Turks as they are in the Ataturk's Turkey, robust and gracious', and notes that his blue eyes make him 'at least half Slav'. See [[Famous Pens on Atatürk — Gunther, Dewey, Nicolson, Coudenhove-Kalergi, West]].
