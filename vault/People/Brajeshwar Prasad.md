# Brajeshwar Prasad

Named Kemal with Hitler, Mussolini and Stalin as builders of 'indirect' democracy in his Constituent Assembly speech against parliamentarism (9 November 1948). See [[Constituent Assembly and Lok Sabha on Atatürk, 1946–1966 — Radhakrishnan, Gour, Kamath, Anthony and Brajeshwar Prasad]].
