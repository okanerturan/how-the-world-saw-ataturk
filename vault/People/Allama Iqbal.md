# Allama Iqbal

Initially inspired, like [[Muhammad Ali Jinnah]], by Atatürk's military victories over Christian
Europe, and celebrated what he saw as a revival of Islam's fighting spirit. But his admiration was
deeply conflicted: the later secular reforms, above all the [[Abolition of the Caliphate]] and the
imposition of a secular legal code, disturbed him profoundly. One of the clearest cases of the split
this vault keeps producing — the general is admired, the legislator is not.
