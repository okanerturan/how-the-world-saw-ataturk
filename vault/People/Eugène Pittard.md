# Eugène Pittard

Geneva anthropologist and professor, spokesman of the foreign scholars at the 2nd Turkish History Congress. Voice in the Turkish Press Directorate's review *La Turquie kémaliste*; see [[La Turquie kémaliste 1935–1938 — Haşim, Wright, Pittard, Ireland and Belge]].
