# Maurice Pernot

French journalist and specialist of the Near East, Revue des Deux Mondes. Portrays the Angora leader as adored by his soldiers and esteemed by generals, a national hero, while noting opponents' charges of dictatorship.

> Moustapha Kemal, adoré par les soldats, est estimé par les chefs, même par ceux dont la valeur technique est peut-être supérieure à la sienne.
> (translation: Moustapha Kemal, adored by the soldiers, is esteemed by the generals, even by those whose technical ability is perhaps superior to his own.)

See [[French Pens on Kemal, 1920–1944 — Herriot, Pernot, Pinon, Delarue-Mardrus and Franklin-Bouillon]].
