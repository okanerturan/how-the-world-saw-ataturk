# Huda Sha'arawi

While conservative clerics decried the destruction of the Caliphate, reformers — especially women —
saw a liberator. By 1934 Turkey had full female suffrage, ahead of France and Italy ([[Women's Suffrage in Turkey]]). Istanbul hosted the 12th Congress of the International Alliance of Women in
1935, and Sha'arawi attended as vice-president, delivering a speech praising Atatürk as a liberator:
while Arab countries were still debating whether women were capable of public life at all, he had
shattered the ceiling for the entire Eastern world.
