# Anthony Eden

As Foreign Secretary he told the Commons on 6 August 1941 that the post-war world would have a noble part for 'the Turkey re-created by the genius of Ataturk', and on 8 January 1942 called Atatürk 'the great leader of modern Turkey'. See [[Westminster on Atatürk, 1941–1957 — Eden, Selwyn Lloyd, Noel-Baker, Wigg, Davies and Hollis]].
