# H.G. Wells

An advocate of a secular world state guided by science and reason. Atatürk had *The Outline of
History* translated into Turkish, read it obsessively, and drew on it in formulating the [[Turkish History Thesis]]. Wells reciprocated, treating the abolition of the Caliphate and forced
secularisation as a monumental triumph of rationalism over dogma and citing Turkey as a vanguard
state.
