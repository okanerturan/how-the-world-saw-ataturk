# François Mitterrand

At the Çankaya dinner in April 1992 he named Atatürk the most valiant protagonist of Turkey's modernising effort and argued Turkey belongs to Europe. See [[American, French and Russian Leaders on Atatürk in Their Own Words]] for the quotes.
