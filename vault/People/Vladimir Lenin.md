# Vladimir Lenin

The alliance was fundamentally pragmatic — both the early Soviets and the Turkish nationalists were
fighting Western powers for survival — but Lenin held genuine respect for Atatürk's strategic
brilliance, reading him not as a communist but as a *bourgeois revolutionary* essential to breaking
British and French imperial grip. He sent the gold and weapons that helped win the [[Turkish War of Independence]], via [[Mikhail Frunze]] and [[Semyon Aralov]].

The return on that investment: Atatürk took the money, won the war, then crushed the Turkish
communist party. See [[Black Sea Incident]], [[Mustafa Suphi]], [[Şefik Hüsnü]].
