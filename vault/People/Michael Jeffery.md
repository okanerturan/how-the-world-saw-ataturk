# Michael Jeffery

Governor-General of Australia. Wrote in the Anıtkabir special book on 2006-04-26: "Australia will never forget Atatürk's symbol of friendship in the years following the war, and to this day his words to the mothers of those who died continue to resonate amongst the Australian people." See [[Anıtkabir Special Book, 2003–2007 — Vajpayee, Olmert, Katsav, Verhofstadt, Balkenende and Others]].
