# José Ortega Munilla

Novelist, academician and long-time editor of El Imparcial, father of the philosopher Ortega y Gasset. In his ABC column 'El triunfo de Turquía' (16 September 1922) he greets Mustafá Kemal as the leader the world war had failed to produce and sees a 'dead people' resurrected. See [[Latin Pens on Mustafa Kemal — Ortega Munilla, Ortega y Gasset, Gramsci and Ledesma Ramos]].
