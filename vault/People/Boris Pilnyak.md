# Boris Pilnyak

Russian novelist and travel writer, shot in 1938. In his Aegean and Levant travel prose 'Повесть о ключах и глине' (1926) a narrator's parenthesis lists Mustafa Kemal Pasha's shelling and burning of Smyrna beside the Dardanelles dead and the Janissary massacres; the year is misdated 1921. Fiction, a narrator's framing, on a sensitive thread. See [[Soviet Pens on Mustafa Kemal — Aralov and Pilnyak]].
