# Hari Vishnu Kamath

Uses Kemal across 1949-66: the ragged army that stood alone (16 May 1949), the Roman script, and the flogging of spitters as a model of drastic penalty. See [[Constituent Assembly and Lok Sabha on Atatürk, 1946–1966 — Radhakrishnan, Gour, Kamath, Anthony and Brajeshwar Prasad]].
