# Eleftherios Venizelos

Architect of the *Megali Idea* and of the Greek invasion of Anatolia that Atatürk violently crushed —
and therefore, on paper, the man with the most reason for lifelong hatred. Instead, a supreme
pragmatist: recognising Greece could not sustain perpetual hostility with a militarised neighbour, he
travelled to Ankara in 1930 to sign a treaty of friendship.

In 1934 he nominated Mustafa Kemal Atatürk for the **Nobel Peace Prize**, arguing that the "New
Turkey" had become a pillar of peace in the Near East and that Atatürk's renunciation of empire was a
monumental achievement. The nomination archive's own annotation (record 1934 24-1, read via its
archived copy) adds a detail the retellings rarely carry: *Kemal made the committee's short list,
but no evaluation was ever written* — the file was left forever incomplete. The 1934 prize went to
Arthur Henderson.
