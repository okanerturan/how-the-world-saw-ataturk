# Rashid Ali al-Kilani

Exiled Iraqi premier courted by Berlin. In 1942 [[Walter Gross]], head of the Nazi racial office,
wrote assuring him the Reich did not consider Arabs a lower caste — on the contrary, racial theory
officially rated Arabs "very high caste" — and that German hostility was directed solely at Jews, who
were "biologically different from the Middle East races." Nazism thereby recast as an ideology
welcoming non-Jewish Semites. Said to be one of only two non-Germans to visit a working death camp,
alongside [[Haj Amin al-Husseini]].
