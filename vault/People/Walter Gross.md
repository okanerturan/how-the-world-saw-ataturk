# Walter Gross

Ran the office that formally decreed the racial laws distinguished not Aryan from non-Aryan but
"Jews" from people of "German and related blood" — the bureaucratic proof of the argument in [[Nazi Racial Hierarchy]] that Jews occupied a category apart rather than the bottom rung. Author of the
1942 letter to [[Rashid Ali al-Kilani]] rating Arabs a "very high caste."
