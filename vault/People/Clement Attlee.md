# Clement Attlee

A Suvla Bay veteran, he wrote in 1954 that the British might have held the Sari Bair height 'but for Mustapha Kemal Pasha being in command of a Turkish Division at the crucial point'. See [[Statesmen on Atatürk in Their Own Words, Memoirs and Speeches]] for the quote and sources.
