# Mahmud Ghunaym

Signs 'Kom Hamada'. In *al-Risala* of 21 November 1938 he printed three verses and an essay on the death. See [[Four Poems on Atatürk — Melbourne, Damascus, Cairo and Hyderabad, 1933–1939]], [[Ahmad Hasan al-Zayyat]], [[Death and State Funeral of Atatürk]].
