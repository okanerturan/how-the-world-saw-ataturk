# Haj Amin al-Husseini

Met Hitler in November 1941 and lived in Nazi Germany for years, making Arabic-language propaganda
broadcasts urging Arabs to back the Axis. With Johann von Leers he grafted Nazi conspiratorial
frameworks onto existing Quranic and jurisprudential language about Jews — the mechanism, per Jeffrey
Herf, that let the message travel where raw race theory could not. He provided religious legitimacy
for recruiting Bosnian Muslims into the Waffen-SS 13th Handschar Division, notably more brutal than
other SS units, and had a significant role in the 1941 Farhud pogrom in Baghdad (~179 Jews killed;
estimates range 149 to 400+).

**The paradox.** Given the Nazi infatuation with Atatürk you would expect their chief Arab
collaborator to share it. He despised him. A deeply conservative Islamist who held the Ottoman Sultan
to be the legitimate Caliph, he read the [[Abolition of the Caliphate]] as a catastrophic betrayal of
Islam and spent the 1920s–30s campaigning across the Arab world against [[Kemalism]] as a Western
poison designed to destroy Muslim unity. Said to be one of only two non-Germans to visit a working
death camp — the other being [[Rashid Ali al-Kilani]].
