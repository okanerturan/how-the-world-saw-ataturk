# René Pinon

French historian and foreign-affairs chronicler of the Revue des Deux Mondes. Treats Kemal as the continuator and accomplice of Enver, Talaat and Djemal, and the real head of a Moscow–Constantinople–Berlin plot against the Allies.

> Mustapha Kemal est le continuateur et le complice des Enver, des Talaat et des Djemal.
> (translation: Mustapha Kemal is the continuator and accomplice of the Envers, Talaats and Djemals.)

See [[French Pens on Kemal, 1920–1944 — Herriot, Pernot, Pinon, Delarue-Mardrus and Franklin-Bouillon]].
