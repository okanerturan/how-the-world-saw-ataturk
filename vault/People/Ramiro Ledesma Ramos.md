# Ramiro Ledesma Ramos

Founder of the weekly La Conquista del Estado (1931), whose 28 March 1931 issue praised 'the genius of the Dictator Mustafá Kemal', and, as 'Roberto Lanzas' in ¿Fascismo en España? (1935), he lists Kemal beside Mussolini, Hitler and Stalin as the face of successful national revolution. See [[Latin Pens on Mustafa Kemal — Ortega Munilla, Ortega y Gasset, Gramsci and Ledesma Ramos]].
