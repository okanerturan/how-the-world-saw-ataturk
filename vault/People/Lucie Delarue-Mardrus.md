# Lucie Delarue-Mardrus

French novelist and poet. Recalls Kemal's unveiling campaign as a wave 'ordered' from above; some Turkish women preferred suicide to the new era. She mocks his cap decree elsewhere.

> au moment de la vague de libération ordonnée par Kémal-Pacha, certaines dames turques ont préféré se suicider qu'entrer dans cette ère nouvelle qui les épouvantait.
> (translation: at the moment of the wave of liberation ordered by Kemal Pasha, some Turkish ladies preferred to kill themselves rather than enter this new era that terrified them.)

See [[French Pens on Kemal, 1920–1944 — Herriot, Pernot, Pinon, Delarue-Mardrus and Franklin-Bouillon]].
