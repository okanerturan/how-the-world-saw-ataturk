# Selwyn Lloyd

In the Cyprus debate of 19 July 1956 he named 'the work of Kemal Ataturk and Venizelos' in building a Greek-Turkish friendship. See [[Westminster on Atatürk, 1941–1957 — Eden, Selwyn Lloyd, Noel-Baker, Wigg, Davies and Hollis]].
