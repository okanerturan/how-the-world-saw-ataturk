# Robert L. F. Sikes

U.S. Representative from Florida (Democrat).

- 1975-10-02: Arguing to lift the Turkish arms embargo, credits 'the strong leadership of Ataturk' with giving the Turks a new concept of national responsibility and progress. See [[Senate and House on Atatürk, 1957–1990 — Powell, Sikes, Mathias and Byrd]].
