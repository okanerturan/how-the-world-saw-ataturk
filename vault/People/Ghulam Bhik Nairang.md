# Ghulam Bhik Nairang

Called Kemal, for a Muslim, 'a veritable Messiah of modern Turkey' and the most prominent figure in Asia. See [[Indian Legislative Assembly Mourns Kemal, 14 November 1938 — Desai, Nairang, Aney, Aikman, Zafar Ali Khan and Sir Abdur Rahim]].
