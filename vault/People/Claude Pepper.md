# Claude Pepper

U.S. Senator from Florida (Democrat), opponent of the Greek-Turkish aid bill.

- 1947-04-17: Cites Atatürk's record to argue communism has no foothold in Turkey, and credits him with building a strong army and government. See [[Congressional Record Pens on Atatürk — Connally, Pepper, Levin, Moynihan, Byrd, Lantos and Others]].
- 1950-12-22: Reporting on a trip, says the 'new Turkey oriented to the West' rests on the 'splendid edifice' Kemal Pasha built after the First World War. See [[Congressional Record Pens on Atatürk — Connally, Pepper, Levin, Moynihan, Byrd, Lantos and Others]].
