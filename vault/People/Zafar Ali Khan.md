# Zafar Ali Khan

Read an elegy to Kemal in the Assembly on 14 November 1938 and asked for a holiday in his memory. See [[Indian Legislative Assembly Mourns Kemal, 14 November 1938 — Desai, Nairang, Aney, Aikman, Zafar Ali Khan and Sir Abdur Rahim]].
