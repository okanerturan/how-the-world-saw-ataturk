# Charles Harington

He called Kemal 'a real soldier' who held the Greeks on the Sakarya, and thought the Allies' own stored arms formed the nucleus of his army. See [[Soldiers on Mustafa Kemal in Their Own Words, 1915–1940]] for the quote and sources.
