# Ma'ruf al-Rusafi

Baghdad's best-known neoclassical poet, a former Ottoman deputy and teacher in Istanbul who returned to Iraq in 1921. His ode to the 'hero of the Greater East' (*Ila batal al-sharq al-akbar*), printed in his *Diwan* (Cairo, 1953, p. 440), names Mustafa Kemal in its first verse, says he beat the Greeks with a victory that left 'the West in tumult', and likens him to a Jesus healing the nation's cracks. Ahmet Farman Saeed Al-Chalaby (2019) notes that many Arab poets passed over the victories in silence. See [[Poets, Reformers and the Black Press on Atatürk, 1921–1935]].
