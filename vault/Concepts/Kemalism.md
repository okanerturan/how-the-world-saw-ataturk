# Kemalism

The exportable ideological product: secular, authoritarian, top-down modernisation carried out by a
military vanguard in a one-party state, aimed at forging a homogenous national identity out of
imperial wreckage. Its "Six Arrows" were republicanism, nationalism, populism, statism
([[Devletçilik]]), secularism and revolutionism.

Copied wholesale by [[Reza Shah Pahlavi]], [[Habib Bourguiba]], [[King Zog I]], [[Plaek Phibunsongkhram]], [[Amanullah Khan]], [[Gamal Abdel Nasser]], [[Pervez Musharraf]] and — with the
polarity reversed — [[Celadet Bedir Khan]] and [[Sati' al-Husri]]. Studied and rejected by [[Mao Zedong]]. Treated as the enemy by [[Sayyid Qutb]], [[Ruhollah Khomeini]], [[Mustafa Sabri Efendi]] and
[[Said Nursi]].
